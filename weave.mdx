You set up campaigns. Each contact has a **weave**: AgentNode takes every campaign they are on and weaves them into one conversation, in your voice, from your number. A birthday text lands on the day. A check-in and a listing update that fall in the same week become one message. Nobody gets two texts because two campaigns asked.

<Info>
  **Example:** Sarah is on *Past clients* and *Birthdays*. Her weave sends one text on her birthday that also asks how the house is treating her, then goes quiet until the next check-in is due.
</Info>

## What the weave decides

For each contact, the weave reads their record, their conversation so far, and every campaign they are on, and works out:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | The next thing to say, written for this person and shaped by the campaign's templates. |
| **When** | A moment a person would pick: inside your sending hours, at least an hour after the last text, and on the day for dated things like birthdays. |
| **Whether to combine** | Two asks that sit together become one text. |
| **Order** | A reply to what the contact just said comes before anything a campaign has queued. |
| **Whether to stop** | If a campaign no longer makes sense for them — they already sold, say — the weave takes them off it and says why in the campaign's activity. |

## Where you see it

Open a contact. Their page opens on the **Weave** tab:

- **Current aim** — what AgentNode is working towards with this person, and the campaigns working them.
- **Steps** — what it has done, ticked off, and what comes next, numbered, each with its day, time, and the text it plans to send.
- **Correct the weave** — a box at the foot. Type "slow down", "I will call instead", or "stop after the next one", and the steps rewrite themselves.

The **Conversation** tab shows the next text from the weave above the reply box, with **Send now instead** if you would rather send it yourself.

## Auto-send

Each weave has an **Auto-send** switch over its steps, on by default.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sends on schedule">
    Every step sends itself at its moment. This is the default.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Waits for your approval">
    Every planned text waits in your Inbox for you to read and send. Nothing goes out on its own for this contact.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

**Settings › Messaging** has the same switch for everyone. Turn it off there and every contact's texts wait for you, whatever their own switch says.

## Pausing

**Pause** sits beside Auto-send. A paused weave plans nothing and sends nothing for that contact until you resume it. Pausing a campaign pauses its part in every contact's weave; pausing a contact pauses only theirs.

<Note>
  Correcting a weave is a one-off instruction, not a chat. AgentNode's questions for you land in the Inbox under **Needs you**, not here. What you say in a correction — what you like, what to avoid — is remembered for every contact.
</Note>
