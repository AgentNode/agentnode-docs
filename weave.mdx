You set up campaigns, each a list of steps. Each contact has a **weave**: AgentNode takes the steps of every campaign they are on and weaves them into one conversation, in your voice, from your number. A birthday text lands on the day. A check-in and a listing update due the same week become one message. Nobody gets two texts because two campaigns asked.

<Info>
  **Example:** Sarah is on *Past clients* and *Birthdays*. Her weave sends one text on her birthday that also asks how the house is treating her, then goes quiet until the next check-in is due.
</Info>

## What the weave decides

For each contact, the weave reads their record, their conversation so far, and the steps of every campaign they are on, and works out:

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **What** | Each text from its step's template, written for this person in your words — or exactly as the template says, when the campaign asks for that. |
| **When** | A moment a person would pick: inside your sending hours, never two texts on one day, and on the day for dated things like birthdays. |
| **Whether to combine** | Two steps due together become one text. |
| **Order** | A reply to what the contact just said comes before anything a campaign has queued. |
| **Whether to wait** | While a contact is replying, their campaigns' steps wait, until the thread has been quiet for a few days or you hand them back. |
| **Whether to stop** | If a campaign no longer makes sense for them — they already sold, say — the weave takes them off it and says why in the campaign's activity. |

The weave never invents a step. What goes out is a campaign's step, or an answer to what the contact said.

## Where you see it

Open a contact. Their page opens on the **Weave** tab:

- **Campaigns** — the campaigns they are on, each with its next step and when, and whether it is running or paused for them. Add them to another, or pause or remove one, here.
- **Steps** — what it has done, ticked off, and what comes next, numbered, each with its day, time, and the text it plans to send.
- **Correct the weave** — a box at the foot. Type "slow down", "I will call instead", or "stop after the next one", and the steps rewrite themselves.

The **Conversation** tab shows the next text from the weave above the reply box, with **Send now instead** if you would rather send it yourself.

## Who presses send

Who presses send is each campaign's choice — sends on its own, approves the first text with you, or waits for you — with a default for every campaign in **Settings › Messaging**. The weave follows it: a held text waits in the Inbox under **Ready to send**, and when two campaigns' texts become one, the stricter of their choices holds.

## Pausing

**Pause** sits at the top of the Weave tab. A paused weave sends nothing for that contact until you resume it. Pausing a campaign pauses its part in every contact's weave; pausing a contact pauses only theirs.

<Note>
  Correcting a weave is a one-off instruction, not a chat. AgentNode's questions for you land in the Inbox under **Needs you**, not here. What you say in a correction — what you like, what to avoid — is remembered for every contact.
</Note>
