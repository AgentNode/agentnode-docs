Every text AgentNode sends is written for one person, from what you know about them, what they have said, and how you like the job done. Once an assistant is on a lead, it sends on its own schedule and answers what comes back without waiting on you, unless it reaches something only you should handle.

## What a text is written from

- **The job and your working preferences**: what the job is for and how you like it done, including preferences aimed at a tag, a source or a list.
- **Your realtor profile**: who you are, your market, and how you sound.
- **The lead's record**: their name, notes, where they came from, and what the tag they carry says assistants should know.
- **The conversation so far**: every text and reply on the thread, and what the assistant has learned about them.

The more of this you provide, the more natural each text reads to the person receiving it.

## Auto-reply

Every text a managed lead sends is answered. Auto-reply reads the thread, the lead's record and the assistant's memory, and answers in your hours when it is sure enough, after the few minutes a person would take: anywhere from 2 to 25 minutes, usually within 10. It books the time a lead agrees to, records what a reply settles, and leaves a draft for you when it is not sure. A lead who asks about price, contracts or legal matters, is upset, or suspects they are texting a machine, is handed to you: automatic replies stop on that thread until you hand it back. See [Guardrails](/guardrails).

## The weave

Every outbound text passes through the **weave** before it goes, whoever wrote it: an assistant, Auto-reply, or you. The weave reads the conversation so far and fits the text into it. If the last text said "Hi, I'm Josh with Acme" and the next one opens the same way, it cuts the introduction. It smooths the join when a text does not follow from the last thing said, so your texts read as one person texting: human, natural, coherent.

It never adds anything. The text's ask, its names, and every number, date, time, price and link stay as written, and after a long silence a fresh hello is left alone. The first text in a conversation is sent as written, and so is a text you looked at and sent anyway after a hold. The thread always shows exactly what went.

## Who presses send

Your assistants' texts send on their own, at their moment. **Settings › Messaging** has one rule on top of that: **Always approve the first text to a new contact**. With it on, the opener waits in your Inbox until you approve it, and follow-ups send as usual. To read every text before it goes, tell [Operator](/operator) to hold them, and each one waits in your Inbox as a draft.

How sure a reply has to be to go on its own is **Confidence** under **Settings › Messaging › Replies**: Low, Medium, High (the default) or Every time. Even at Every time, a reply AgentNode cannot vouch for at all waits for you.

A text AgentNode was not sure enough to send waits in the Inbox too, with the reason, and you can send it, edit it, or discard it. See [the Inbox](/inbox).

## When texts go

- Nothing sends in **quiet hours**; a text due then waits for morning.
- One scheduled text a day per person, and an hour between texts while they say nothing. Answers to a reply are not held back.
- A job's texts hold while the lead is talking with Auto-reply or has a meeting booked, and pick up once the thread has been quiet for a few days.
- Nobody gets two texts because two jobs asked.

## Personalisation

AgentNode does not send generic follow-ups. Here is how a typical exchange can look:

> "Hi Marcus, thinking of you. The market in Eastside has shifted quite a bit since we last spoke. Worth a quick catch-up?" *(sent by Lead Nurture)*

> "Oh wow, yeah, we've actually been thinking about it lately."

> "Great to hear. I have Thursday at 2:00 or Friday morning free. Which suits you?" *(sent by Auto-reply)*

## On-demand CMA generator

When a lead wants to know what is happening in their local market, AgentNode can generate a Comparative Market Analysis on demand, so you and the assistant have current data to share before you pick up the phone.
