Every text AgentNode sends is written for one person, from what you know about them, what they have said, and how you like the job done. Once an assistant is on a lead, it sends on its own schedule and answers what comes back without waiting on you, unless it reaches something only you should handle.

## What a text is written from

- **The job and your working preferences**: what the job is for and how you like it done, including preferences aimed at a tag, a source or a list.
- **Your realtor profile**: the facts about you and your business it may quote when a lead asks.
- **The lead's record**: their name, notes and where they came from.
- **The conversation**: every text and reply so far, the texts still planned for them, and what the assistant has learned about them.

The more of this you provide, the more natural each text reads to the person receiving it.

## Auto-reply

Every text a managed lead sends is answered. Auto-reply reads the conversation, the lead's record and the assistant's memory, and answers in your hours when it is sure enough, after the few minutes a person would take: anywhere from 2 to 25 minutes, usually within 10. While it waits, the conversation shows the reply and when it will send; you can send it now, or send your own words in its place. It books the time a lead agrees to, records what a reply settles, and leaves a draft for you when it is not sure. A lead who asks about price, contracts or legal matters, is upset, or suspects they are texting a machine, is handed to you: automatic replies stop for that conversation until you press **Hand it back**. See [Guardrails](/guardrails).

## Written into the conversation

Each text is written for the conversation it joins: everything said so far, and the texts still planned for that person by any assistant. So planned texts blend with what was said and with each other. If your last text said "Hi, I'm Josh with Acme", the next one does not introduce you again or repeat what another planned text says.

If something changes before a planned text goes, like the lead writes, you text them yourself, or another text reaches them first, that text is not sent as written. The assistant writes it again to fit the conversation as it is now. If it was already waiting to send, it is taken back, and the conversation says why.

Texts you write go exactly as you typed them.

## Who presses send

Your assistants' texts send on their own, at their moment, while **Reply for me** is on. It starts on. To read every text before it goes, turn it off under **Settings › Messaging › Replies**. Each one then waits in your Inbox as a draft. **Settings › Messaging** has one rule on top of that: **Manually approve the first plan for each lead**. With it on, each lead's first plan waits in your Inbox until you approve it, and plans after it go on their own. A first plan you approved goes as planned, without asking you again, even with **Reply for me** off. See [Approving first plans](/contacts#approving-first-plans).

**Confidence**, also under **Replies**, sets how much you trust AgentNode to send without you: Low, Medium, High (the default) or Every time. The higher you set it, the more replies go on their own. The lower, the more wait for your OK. Even at Every time, a reply AgentNode has no good answer for waits for you.

A text AgentNode was not sure enough to send waits in the Inbox too, with the reason, and you can send it, edit it, or discard it. See [the Inbox](/inbox).

## When texts go

- Nothing sends in **quiet hours** (9 PM to 8 AM). A text due then waits in your Inbox for your OK, unless you changed that in Settings. One in a first plan you approved goes the next morning instead.
- There is no set number of texts a day. The assistants decide together what goes and when, and a text they hold back shows on the contact's page with why, and when it is looked at again.
- A job's texts hold while the lead is talking with Auto-reply or has a meeting booked, and pick up once the conversation has been quiet for a few days.
- Nobody gets two texts because two jobs asked.

## Personalization

AgentNode does not send generic follow-ups. Here is how a typical exchange can look:

> "Hi Marcus, thinking of you. The market in Eastside has shifted quite a bit since we last spoke. Worth a quick catch-up?" *(sent by Lead Nurture)*

> "Oh wow, yeah, we've actually been thinking about it lately."

> "Great to hear. I have Thursday at 2:00 or Friday morning free. Which suits you?" *(sent by Auto-reply)*

## When a lead asks what their home is worth

An assistant never guesses a figure. A lead who asks what their place is worth is handed to you. Ask AgentNode to draft the answer: it looks the property up, and the draft carries a real estimate, the range around it, and the sales behind it. Or ask it directly, in Operator: "what's 14 Alder Court worth?"
