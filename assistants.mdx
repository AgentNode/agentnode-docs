AgentNode comes with three assistants. Each runs a set of **jobs**, standing pieces of work it does for the people it looks after, and each has a page of its own under **Your assistants** in the left nav, with a chat where you talk to it.

| Assistant | Looks after | Jobs |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Nurture** | New and active leads | New lead, Auto-reply, Follow-up, Re-engagement |
| **Client Relations** | Past clients and your sphere | Birthdays and anniversaries, Review request, Referral ask |
| **Appointments** | Everyone's meetings | Book a call, Missed appointment, Unconfirmed booking |

Every lead has one owner, Lead Nurture, Client Relations or you. Two things work every lead whoever owns them: Auto-reply answers what they text back, and Appointments looks after their meetings.

## An assistant's page

Open an assistant from the left nav.

- **Overview** opens first: "Lead Nurture is looking after 412 people", with this week's texts, replies and bookings. **What it is doing** lists today's work in a sentence each. **What it has learned** offers changes it would make, with an **Apply** button on each. **Needs your attention** lists the threads and questions waiting on you, with a link to open them in the Inbox.
- **Talk to the assistant** sits beside it: a chat where you ask what it is doing, tell it how you like things done, or give it a one-off instruction. "Why did Marcus go quiet?", "Pause Zillow leads today", "Wait three days between follow-ups".
- **Jobs**, in the left column, one row per job with a green dot when it is on.
- **What it knows**: **People**, the leads it looks after (for Appointments, the people whose meetings it is working); **Memory**, what it has learned about each person: facts, preferences, intent and behaviour; **Outcomes**, what worked, what did not, timing and response patterns, and suggested improvements you can apply.

## Jobs

A job page shows what the job does in a sentence, an **On** switch, and one card, **Working Preferences**: how you like this job done, and which integrations it uses.

### Working preferences

Every row in the card is one preference. Some are things the job does through an integration: **Book the call in your calendar · Google Calendar**, **Log the touch · Follow Up Boss**, **Remind you 10 minutes before · Push to your mobile**. Each has a switch, and a dimmed row is off. The rest are how you like it done, one line each: "Mention the exact listing they asked about in the first text", "Offer two viewing times, never an open question about when", "Sign off as Josh, not Sky Realty". Add one in the field at the foot of the card and press Enter; the **x** on a row removes it.

Every row has a tool dropdown. A preference you typed reads **No tool** until you pick one of your connected integrations (Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Follow Up Boss, MLS, ShowingTime, Zoom, Gmail, Push to your mobile); **Add integration** at the foot of the menu opens Settings › Integrations. Texting is never a tool: every job texts on its own.

A preference can be aimed at some people only. Type `#` for a tag, `@` for a source, or `~` for a list, or pick one from the chips under the field: "If `#sky-realty`, send from the Sky Realty number and sign off as Sky Realty", "If from `@zillow`, mention Zillow". A preference applies only to people who match every tag, source and list it names; everything else applies to everyone on the job.

## Lead Nurture

- **New lead**: when a new lead arrives, texts them within minutes in your hours, learns what they are looking for and follows up. A lead without consent on file is a question to you instead.
- **Auto-reply**: answers every text a lead sends, whoever owns them, when it is sure enough of the answer, and books the time they agree to. Anything it is not sure of comes to you.
- **Follow-up**: follows up with people who went quiet mid-conversation, at the spacing you set.
- **Re-engagement**: wakes up leads who have been quiet for 30 days or more, strongest reason first, within your daily limit.

## Client Relations

- **Birthdays and anniversaries**: a text on the day, each year. It asks you for the dates it does not have, one by one or as a CSV.
- **Review request**: asks for a review after a closing, with your review link.
- **Referral ask**: asks past clients for an introduction, as often as you set.

Client Relations' jobs wait while a lead is talking with Auto-reply or has a meeting booked, and pick up once the thread has been quiet for a few days.

## Appointments

- **Book a call**: when a lead says yes to a call, or Lead Nurture hands them over, offers two times from your calendar and books the call. Three attempts; stops when the call is booked or they opt out.
- **Missed appointment**: offers two new times within minutes of a no-show or a cancellation, until one is booked. A second miss in a row comes to you instead.
- **Unconfirmed booking**: reminds anyone who has not confirmed, outside quiet hours.

Appointments owns no leads. It works every meeting for whoever owns the lead, and never takes the lead from them.

## Switching off and pausing

Switch a job off on its page and it plans nothing more; what it already sent stays on the thread. Tell an assistant to pause someone in its chat, or pause a contact on their page, and nothing is sent to them until you resume. An assistant never texts anyone who has opted out.
