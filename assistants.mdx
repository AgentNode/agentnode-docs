AgentNode comes with three assistants. Each runs a set of **jobs**, tasks it keeps doing for your leads, and each has a page of its own under **Your assistants** in the menu on the left.

| Assistant | Works on | Jobs |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Nurture** | New and active leads | New lead, Auto-reply, Book a call, Follow-up, Re-engagement |
| **Client Relations** | Past clients and your sphere | Birthdays, Home anniversaries, Review request, Referral ask |
| **Appointment Recovery** | Everyone's meetings | Missed appointment, Unconfirmed booking |

The assistants share every lead and work as a team. When something happens with a managed lead, like a new arrival, a reply, a meeting or a silence, every assistant it matters to hears about it. Before any text goes out, they check with each other, so a lead never gets two texts at once. If a lead sends two texts in a row, they get one reply that answers both. Switch **Managed** off on a lead's page and no assistant acts for them.

## An assistant's page

Open an assistant from the menu on the left. Its page has a menu of its own: **Assistant** at the top, then its **Jobs**, each with a green dot while it is on.

**Assistant** shows how the last 30 days went:

- **Lead Nurture**: **Reply rate**, compared with the 30 days before; **Leads booked**, of the new leads that arrived; and **Active conversations**, with how many are waiting on a follow-up.
- **Client Relations**: **Reply rate**, compared with the 30 days before; **Clients reached**, with how many texts it delivered; and **Clients booked**, of the clients it reached, a meeting booked after its first text.
- **Appointment Recovery**: **Rebooked**, of the meetings your leads missed or called off, and **Time to rebook**, the median time from a miss to a new booking.

To ask an assistant what it is doing, or tell it something, ask [Operator](/operator).

## Jobs

A job's page says what the job does, with a switch: **On**, or **Paused** when you switch it off. Below it are two cards: **Add-ons**, extra things the job can do, and **Working preferences**, how you like it done, in your words.

### Add-ons

Add-ons are built by AgentNode, each using one of your tools: **Book a viewing** with Google Calendar, **Look up a listing** with the MLS, **Log the touch** with Follow Up Boss. Add-ons that already work show the app they use. Ones that do not work yet say **coming soon**. Each has a switch, and a faded row is off. Press one to open its settings:

- **Integration**: which of your connected apps it uses. **Add integration** opens Settings › Integrations.
- **When it runs**: **Automatically**, whenever the job needs it, or **Ask me first**, in the Inbox before it uses it.
- **Instructions**: anything it should know, such as "Only for listings under 800k. Mention the school district."

Nothing changes until you press **Save**, which saves them together.

When a lead says yes to a time, a job whose booking add-on is on (**Book a viewing**, **Offer and book times**, **Book the call in your calendar** or **Rebook the viewing**) books it on your calendar and texts them to confirm, as long as the time is still free and they have no meeting ahead. If automatic replies are off (in Settings, or for that conversation), or the add-on is set to **Ask me first**, the booking waits in your [Inbox](/inbox) with its confirming text. An assistant never moves or cancels a meeting.

### Working preferences

Every row is one preference, in your words: "Mention the exact listing they asked about in the first text", "Offer two viewing times, never an open question about when", "Sign off as Josh, not Sky Realty". A line under each says who added it. Describe one in the box at the foot, pick a tool beside it if it needs one, and press **Add**. The **x** on a row removes it, and **Undo** puts it back.

A preference can be aimed at some people only. Type `#` for a tag, `@` for a source, or `~` for a list, and pick from the names that appear as you type: "If `#sky-realty`, send from the Sky Realty number and sign off as Sky Realty", "If from `@zillow`, mention Zillow". If a preference names more than one tag, source or list, it only applies to people who match all of them. A preference that names none applies to everyone on the job.

You do not need to connect anything for texting: every job can already text.

## Lead Nurture

- **New lead**: when a new inquiry arrives, texts them within minutes in your hours, learns what they are looking for and follows up. A new inquiry is someone who has just reached out: someone who comes in from Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel the moment they are added there, is posted in your Slack leads channel, or you add through Operator as a new lead. People from a CSV file or the once-a-day CRM check are not. If there is no permission to text them on file, AgentNode asks you first.
- **Auto-reply**: answers every text a lead sends, a few minutes later as a person would, when it is sure enough of the answer, and books the time they agree to. Anything it is not sure of comes to you.
- **Book a call**: when a lead says yes to a call, offers two times from your calendar and books the call. Three attempts; stops when the call is booked or they opt out.
- **Follow-up**: restarts the conversation, with a new reason each time, when a lead goes quiet mid-conversation. Each morning AgentNode looks for leads who wrote, got an answer and have not written since, and Follow-up picks them up from the morning after.
- **Re-engagement**: asks leads who have been quiet 30 days or more one low-pressure question: are they still looking? If they do not answer, it nudges once more.

Lead Nurture stops reaching out to anyone with a client tag. Auto-reply still answers them.

## Client Relations

Client Relations works for the people with a client tag, such as `#past-clients`, in AgentNode or in your CRM. Give a managed lead such a tag and Client Relations takes them up.

- **Birthdays**: a text on their birthday, each year. It asks you for the dates it does not have, one by one or as a CSV.
- **Home anniversaries**: a text on the anniversary of the day they got their keys, each year. It asks you for the dates it does not have the same way.
- **Review request**: asks for a review after a closing, with your review link.
- **Referral ask**: asks past clients for an introduction, once a quarter.

Client Relations' jobs wait while a lead is talking with Auto-reply or has a meeting booked, and pick up once the conversation has been quiet for a few days.

## Appointment Recovery

- **Missed appointment**: offers two new times within minutes of a no-show, or of a lead calling a meeting off (by their text, by you telling Operator, or by your answer when Unconfirmed booking asks), until one is booked. A meeting you cancel yourself is not rebooked. A second miss in a row comes to you instead.
- **Unconfirmed booking**: reminds anyone who has not confirmed, outside quiet hours. When your calendar gives a meeting a video link, it texts the lead that link the same way, and each reminder carries it; with the job off, or for a lead AgentNode does not manage, send the link yourself. No reminder goes for a meeting the lead called off, even while it is still on your calendar. About 15 minutes after a meeting ends, if nothing says whether the lead came, it asks you in the Inbox. Tell it another rule, like "check an hour after", and it follows that instead. Answer that they didn't, and Missed appointment offers them new times.

## Switching off and pausing

Switch a job off on its page and it sends nothing more; what it already sent stays in the conversation. Switch **Managed** off on a contact's page and no assistant texts them until you turn it back on. An assistant never texts anyone who has opted out.
