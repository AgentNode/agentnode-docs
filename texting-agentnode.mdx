You can talk to AgentNode by text message, from your own phone, the same way you type to [Operator](/operator) in the app. Text it a name and number to add a contact, send a photo of a business card, ask what needs you, or tell it what to do. It texts you back.

## Turn it on

1. Save your mobile number under **Settings › Account**, in **Phone number**. AgentNode only answers texts from that number.
2. Go to **Settings › Channels** and press **Turn on** under **SMS channel**.
3. AgentNode texts you hello from its own number: how to add a contact by text or photo, and a link to the app. Save the number as **AgentNode**, so its texts show with its name. On most phones its contact card comes with the hello, and one tap saves it.

If AgentNode's team set up your account on a call with you, this is already done: the hello came as soon as you paid.

## What you can text it

Anything you would type to Operator in the app. For example:

- **Add a contact**: "Add Jane Cooper 310 555 0199, met at the Fairview open house".
- **Send a photo**: a business card, a screenshot of a lead, or a contact card. AgentNode reads it and adds the person. Up to 5 photos a text.
- **Ask**: "Who needs me today?", "What did Sam say last?", "What's booked tomorrow?"
- **Text a lead**: "Text Sam I'm running 10 minutes late". Consent and opt-outs still apply.
- **Set up a job**: "Email me a report on my leads every morning at 8". See [Jobs](/operator#jobs).
- **Pass on news**: "Pat is ready to look again in the spring".

A day after the hello, AgentNode texts you a few ideas like these to try.

Your texts and its replies land in the same Operator chat you see in the app and on the [iPhone app](/iphone-app), so you can pick up anywhere.

## Your morning report

At 9 every weekday morning, your time, AgentNode texts you what is waiting on you in the Inbox's **Needs you**: how many things, who they are about and what it stopped on. Reply to go through them by text, or open the link to answer them in the app. On a morning nothing is waiting, it sends nothing.

It comes as well as the morning email, and stops when you text **STOP**.

## What it will not do by text

A text can come from anyone who knows your number, so a few things that cannot be undone only happen in the app:

- Deleting contacts, conversations, tags or jobs.
- Starting one of your CRM's automations, such as a Follow Up Boss action plan.

Ask for one of these by text and AgentNode tells you to do it in the app.

## Turn it off

- Text **STOP** to AgentNode's number. It stops texting you straight away. Text **START** to turn it back on.
- Or press **Turn off** under **SMS channel** in **Settings › Channels**, and turn it on again there.

<Note>
  Texts to and from AgentNode cost credits, like any text. See [Billing](/settings#billing).
</Note>
