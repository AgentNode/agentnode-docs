The words you will meet in AgentNode, in plain English.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Assistant** | One of AgentNode's three AI helpers: Lead Nurture, Client Relations and Appointment Recovery. Each has a set of jobs. |
| **Job** | One standing piece of work an assistant does, like **New lead** or **Birthdays**. Each can be switched on or off and told how you like it done. |
| **Working preferences** | Your instructions for a job, in your own words: "Offer two times, never an open question." |
| **Add-on** | An extra thing a job can do with one of your tools, like booking a viewing on your calendar. |
| **Operator** | Your home screen, and the chat where you ask or tell AgentNode anything. The **AgentNode** button at the bottom right of every screen opens the same chat. |
| **Inbox** | Everything waiting on you: questions from assistants, texts held for your approval, and conversations handed to you. |
| **Lead, contact** | A person in your Contacts. A lead is someone who might buy or sell; a contact is anyone, past clients included. |
| **Managed** | Whether AgentNode's assistants work on a contact. On, they text and answer for you. Off, AgentNode leaves them alone. Everyone you add starts managed. |
| **Tag** | A label on a contact, like `#zillow-leads` or `#past-clients`. A contact can carry many. A tag that says client moves them to Client Relations. |
| **Source** | Where a contact came from: a spreadsheet upload, Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, Slack, or added by hand. |
| **Consent on file** | Your confirmation that a person agreed to hear from you by text. You give it when you import or add someone. AgentNode never texts anyone without it. |
| **Opt-out** | A lead replying STOP (or END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, STOPALL). AgentNode never texts them again, and this cannot be undone in the app. |
| **Quiet hours** | Hours when nothing sends on its own. Set under **Settings › Messaging**. |
| **Credits** | What your plan includes to pay for texts, calls, AI and your number. One credit is one dollar. See **Settings › Billing**. |
| **Carrier registration** | US phone carriers require every business that sends texts to be registered first. AgentNode files it for you from your business details. Approval takes 1 to 3 business days; your number texts under AgentNode's own approval in the meantime. |
| **EIN** | Your business's nine-digit tax ID from the IRS, like 12-3456789. It is on the IRS letter (CP 575) you got when it was issued. The carriers register a business by it. |
| **CRM** | The system where you keep your leads, such as Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel. |
| **CSV file** | A plain spreadsheet file. Every CRM, Google Sheets and Excel can save one. It is how you upload a list of contacts. |
| **Auto-import** | Sources that bring new leads into AgentNode on their own: Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel and a Slack channel. Under **Contacts**. |
| **Realtor profile** | Facts about you and your business that assistants may quote when a lead asks. Under **Settings**. |
| **Signature** | The name the first text to each person signs off with. |
