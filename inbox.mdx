The **Inbox** is where your day starts. Everything that needs a person lands here: a fact an assistant does not have, a text waiting for your OK, a conversation handed to you because a lead needs a person. Nothing an assistant is still handling on its own appears here.

## What lands in the Inbox

  <Card title="A question from an assistant" icon="circle-question">
    "Is 14 Alder Court still at 1.2M?", "When is the Fairview open house?" The assistant asks about the lead it is working on, and picks up as soon as you answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A text held for you" icon="hand">
    A text an assistant wrote but did not send: with **Reply for me** off, or when it was not sure enough to send it. Read it, then send it, edit it, or discard it. If you send the same words yourself from the conversation, it is marked sent. If you write your own text instead, it is dropped.
  </Card>
  <Card title="First plans waiting for your approval" icon="clipboard-check">
    With **Manually approve the first plan for each lead** on, each lead's first plan waits for you, and nothing reaches them until you approve it. However many wait, they share one line: approve them all, or review them one at a time. **Clear all** leaves it. See [Approving first plans](/contacts#approving-first-plans).
  </Card>
  <Card title="A conversation handed to you" icon="user">
    A lead asked about price or contracts, is upset, suspects they are texting a machine, or asked something the assistant could not answer. AgentNode pauses texting them, so you can answer them yourself. **Keep it paused** leaves them to you; **Carry on** has AgentNode pick the conversation back up. **Let me answer** texts them from you and keeps it paused. If AgentNode was holding a text there, it then goes if your settings allow, or waits here again.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A booking waiting for your yes" icon="calendar-check">
    A lead said yes to a time while **Reply for me** is off or texting them is paused, or the booking add-on asks first. **Book it** books the meeting and sends the confirming text; **Not now** leaves it unbooked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A text that came in at your limit" icon="hourglass-half">
    A lead texted while AgentNode is paused because today's [Daily AI limit](/settings#billing) is reached, or your credits are spent. **Answer now** answers them anyway; **Wait** leaves it until midnight, or until your credits renew.
  </Card>

## Reading the list

Each item says which assistant raised it, or which lead replied, what is needed, and how long it has waited. Open one to see the lead's history, what was asked, and the answers you can give.

Answering wakes the assistant that asked, and it picks up with that lead where it stopped. What you type in the answer box goes to the assistant, never to the lead: if it calls for a text, the assistant writes one. To answer the lead yourself instead, press **Let me answer** with their name, like **Let me answer Dana**, and write your text: it goes to them from your number, and the assistant that asked hears what you said. What you write in your own words is learned too: anything about the lead is kept for them, and a rule for all your leads, like "never offer Saturdays", is kept for every assistant. See [How it learns you](/learning).

## Clearing the Inbox

**Clear all** empties the Inbox in one go. Everything waiting leaves it, as if you had ignored each one: nothing is sent, answered or booked. Held texts stay held, and an assistant that still needs an answer can ask again later. Changed your mind? **Undo** puts back what was cleared.

## Conversations and Calendar

A lead's conversation is on their page in **Contacts**, and anything in the app that opens a conversation opens it there. **Conversations**, under Contacts in the menu, lists every conversation, the latest message first: press one to read it, then **Open conversation** to reply. Read it there and reply yourself: your text goes from your number, as if you had texted them directly, and the assistant keeps handling the conversation unless you have taken it over. The next text planned for them is shown above your reply, and their whole plan is on their **Overview**. Texting a lead from their conversation does not answer what an assistant asked you about them: answer that in the Inbox, where **Let me answer** texts them too. The AI logo on any of AgentNode's messages is where you say what to correct, or why it was wrong; AgentNode remembers it for every assistant. See [A contact's page](/contacts#a-contacts-page).

**Calendar** is in the menu on the left, under **Contacts**: the appointments your leads have booked, the most recent first, then the week ahead by day.

<Note>
  You never have to go looking for a question. Everything an assistant asks lands in the Inbox while it waits.
