The **Inbox** tab on your home screen is where your day starts. Everything that needs a person lands here: a fact an assistant does not have, a text waiting for your approval, a thread handed to you because a lead needs a human. Each row says who raised it, what is needed, and how long it has waited. Nothing an assistant is still handling on its own appears here.

## What lands in the Inbox

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A question from an assistant" icon="circle-question">
    "Is 14 Alder Court still at 1.2M?", "When is the Fairview open house?" The assistant asks once, however many people are waiting on the answer, and your one answer serves them all.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A text held for you" icon="hand">
    A text an assistant wrote but did not send: the first text to a new contact when you approve those, or a text it was not sure enough to send. Read it, then send it, edit it, or discard it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A thread handed to you" icon="user">
    A lead asked about price or contracts, is upset, suspects they are texting a machine, or asked something the assistant could not answer. Automatic replies stop on that thread until you hand it back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A decision only you can make" icon="scale">
    "Two goals want the same 41 people": two jobs could start for the same contacts, such as an import that fits both New lead and Re-engagement. Your answer decides for the whole group.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Reading the list

Each row shows **From**, **What's needed** and **Waiting**. From is the assistant that raised it, with its icon: Lead Nurture, Client Relations, Appointments or Operator. A reply from a lead shows the lead's name instead. Click a row to open it on its own, with the lead's history, what was asked, and the answers you can give.

Answering wakes the assistant that asked. It picks up where it stopped, for every person who was waiting on that answer.

## Conversations and Bookings

Two more pages in the left nav, under **Your data**, carry the rest:

- **Conversations**: every thread, most recent first, with the last thing said. Open one to read it and reply yourself. Your text goes from your number, as if you had texted them directly, and the assistant keeps handling the thread unless you have taken it over. The AI logo on any message opens a box for what to correct, or why it was wrong; AgentNode remembers it for every assistant.
- **Bookings**: the appointments your leads have booked, the most recent first, then the week ahead by day.

<Note>
  You never have to go looking for a question. Anything an assistant asks in its own chat also lands in the Inbox while it waits, and an answer in either place counts the same.
</Note>
