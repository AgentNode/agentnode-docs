This page summarises the key points of AgentNode's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service so you can understand your rights and obligations at a glance. AgentNode is owned and operated by Whiteworth Inc. The full documents govern your use of the platform and are available at [goagentnode.com](https://goagentnode.com). Where a summary and the full document differ, the full document controls.

## Privacy policy highlights

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What data AgentNode collects">
    AgentNode collects several categories of information depending on how you use the platform:

    - **Account information** — your name, business name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
    - **Contact lists you import** — names, phone numbers, notes, tags, and any other fields you include in a CSV import or CRM integration.
    - **Conversation data** — message content, delivery status, opt-in and opt-out records, and conversation history between you and your leads.
    - **Usage data** — pages visited, features used, session information, device and browser data, and other information collected automatically when you use the platform.
    - **Billing information** — subscription status, payment method details (processed by Stripe), and transaction history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How AgentNode uses your data">
    AgentNode uses your data to operate and improve the service. Specific uses include:

    - Providing, maintaining, and securing the platform and your account.
    - Generating and sending AI-assisted messages on your behalf based on your messaging preferences.
    - Processing payments, managing subscriptions, and administering credits.
    - Communicating with you about your account, service changes, and support requests.
    - Analysing usage patterns in aggregate to improve AI features and platform performance.
    - Complying with legal, regulatory, and carrier obligations.

    AgentNode does not sell your personal information or your leads' contact data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI features and third-party providers">
    AgentNode uses third-party AI providers — including Anthropic and OpenRouter — to generate messages and assist with automations. When the platform drafts a message for a lead, your contact data, messaging preferences, and relevant conversation history may be processed by these providers to produce that output.

    The specific providers AgentNode uses may change over time as the platform evolves. AI-generated content may occasionally be inaccurate or unsuitable — you are responsible for supervising and, where appropriate, reviewing what the AI sends on your behalf.

    AgentNode's infrastructure providers include Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Vercel. SMS and telephony are handled through SignalHouse. Property data services are provided by RentCast and Realie.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SMS and mobile messaging">
    AgentNode processes SMS data specifically to operate the messaging service. This includes mobile phone numbers, message content, delivery and error status, opt-in and opt-out records, consent source and timestamp, and campaign configuration data.

    AgentNode does not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile phone numbers, text-message opt-in data, or SMS consent to third parties for their own marketing, lead generation, or commercial purposes. SMS consent is not transferable between campaigns, brands, or senders.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data retention">
    AgentNode retains your data for as long as your account is active and for a period after account deletion as required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

    Consent records, opt-out records, and message history may be retained for longer periods to satisfy carrier, registry, and legal recordkeeping requirements. When data is no longer needed, AgentNode deletes, anonymises, or aggregates it in line with its standard practices.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your rights">
    Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information AgentNode holds about you. You may also have the right to opt out of certain types of data processing.

    To submit a privacy request, email [legal@goagentnode.com](mailto:legal@goagentnode.com) with the subject line **"Privacy Request"** and include enough detail to identify the information at issue. AgentNode will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Terms of service highlights

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Subscription and credits">
    AgentNode operates on a monthly subscription basis. Each subscription period includes an allocation of credits, which are consumed by outgoing messages and other billable actions. Credits expire at the end of each subscription period, do not roll over, and have no cash value.

    Your subscription renews automatically each month unless you provide notice of non-renewal before the Non-Renewal Notice Date (the day before the last day of your current period, measured in Eastern Time). You can view your current plan, credit balance, and usage from your account dashboard.

    AgentNode may change subscription pricing or credit consumption rates, but only after giving you at least 30 days' written notice before the renewal date on which the change takes effect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Permitted use">
    AgentNode is for licensed real estate professionals conducting legitimate lead follow-up in connection with their real estate business. You may only use the platform for your own real estate activity — not on behalf of unrelated businesses, and not to resell or white-label access to others.

    Prohibited uses include contacting purchased or scraped lead lists, sending spam or deceptive messages, using the platform for debt collection or other restricted content categories, and any conduct that violates fair housing laws, consumer protection regulations, or carrier and registry requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messaging compliance obligations">
    You are the sender of every message AgentNode transmits on your behalf. You are solely responsible for ensuring your contact list has appropriate consent, that your campaigns are registered correctly, and that your outreach complies with the TCPA and applicable state laws.

    AgentNode provides tools to support compliance — including automatic opt-out processing, quiet-hour settings, and consent recording at import — but these are convenience tools. They do not constitute legal advice and do not guarantee that your messaging practices are legally sufficient. You remain responsible for your compliance programme.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Human escalation requirement">
    AgentNode is designed to hand off conversations to you when a human response is needed. You are required to be available to handle those escalated conversations in a timely manner, particularly for complaints, consent questions, opt-out requests, fair-housing-sensitive topics, and any matter involving offers, transaction terms, or regulated real estate communications.

    AgentNode assists you — it does not replace your professional judgement or your obligations as a licensed real estate professional. Provider does not act as your broker, agent, fiduciary, or legal adviser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dispute resolution">
    Disputes between you and Whiteworth Inc. are governed by the law of the State of Delaware. Before commencing any formal proceeding, both parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution for 60 days after written notice of the dispute.

    Disputes that cannot be resolved informally are submitted to binding individual arbitration, administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, seated in Delaware. Class actions, collective actions, and representative proceedings are waived to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law — claims may only be brought in an individual capacity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
