This page summarizes the key points of AgentNode's Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Data Processing Agreement so you can understand your rights and obligations at a glance. AgentNode is owned and operated by Whiteworth Inc. The full documents govern your use of the platform: the [Privacy Policy](https://goagentnode.com/privacy), the [Terms of Service](https://goagentnode.com/terms) and the [Data Processing Agreement](https://goagentnode.com/dpa). Where a summary and the full document differ, the full document controls.

## Privacy policy highlights

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What data AgentNode collects">
    AgentNode collects several categories of information depending on how you use the platform:

    - **Account information** — your name, business name, email address, phone number, and account credentials.
    - **Contact lists you import** — names, phone numbers, notes, tags, and any other fields you include in a CSV import or CRM integration.
    - **Conversation data** — message content, delivery status, opt-in and opt-out records, and conversation history between you and your leads.
    - **Calls** — call recordings, transcripts and call details. Calls you make through AgentNode are recorded and transcribed by default.
    - **Usage data** — pages visited, features used, session information, device and browser data, and other information collected automatically when you use the platform.
    - **Billing information** — subscription status, payment method details (processed by Stripe), and transaction history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How AgentNode uses your data">
    AgentNode uses your data to operate and improve the service. Specific uses include:

    - Providing, maintaining, and securing the platform and your account.
    - Generating and sending AI-assisted messages on your behalf based on your messaging preferences.
    - Processing payments, managing subscriptions, and administering credits.
    - Communicating with you about your account, service changes, and support requests.
    - Analyzing usage patterns in aggregate to improve AI features and platform performance.
    - Complying with legal, regulatory, and carrier obligations.

    AgentNode does not sell your personal information or your leads' contact data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="AI features and third-party providers">
    AgentNode uses third-party AI providers to write messages, assist with automations and work with call transcripts. When the platform drafts a message for a lead, your contact data, messaging preferences, and relevant conversation history may be processed by these providers to produce that output.

    The specific providers AgentNode uses may change over time as the platform evolves. AI-generated content may occasionally be inaccurate or unsuitable — you are responsible for supervising and, where appropriate, reviewing what the AI sends on your behalf.

    AgentNode may use data from the service to train and improve the AI in its products, including AI from other companies that is part of AgentNode, but only once it is combined with other data and, as far as it reasonably can, stripped of what identifies anyone.

    The companies that store and process your contacts' data for AgentNode, such as its hosting, texting and calling, payment and AI providers, are listed in the [Data Processing Agreement](https://goagentnode.com/dpa). Where it names a kind of provider rather than a company, email [legal@goagentnode.com](mailto:legal@goagentnode.com) for the name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your contacts' data">
    When AgentNode handles your contacts' personal information for you, it acts on your instructions, under the [Data Processing Agreement](https://goagentnode.com/dpa). The agreement is part of the Terms of Service: you accept it when you accept the Terms, with nothing to sign. Where the agreement and the Privacy Policy differ about your contacts' data, the agreement controls.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="SMS and mobile messaging">
    AgentNode processes SMS data specifically to operate the messaging service. This includes mobile phone numbers, message content, delivery and error status, opt-in and opt-out records, where and when consent was given, and your texting registration details.

    AgentNode does not sell, rent, share, or transfer mobile phone numbers, text-message opt-in data, or SMS consent to third parties for their own marketing, lead generation, or commercial purposes. Consent to get texts from one sender never carries over to another.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Data retention">
    AgentNode keeps your data while your account is active, and afterward only as long as the law, the Terms and the Data Processing Agreement allow, for example for dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance.

    Consent records, opt-out records, and message history may be kept longer to satisfy carrier, registry, and legal recordkeeping requirements. When data is no longer needed, AgentNode deletes, anonymizes, or aggregates it.

    **Delete account**, in **Settings › Account**, permanently removes your contacts, conversations and settings, releases your texting numbers and deletes your carrier registration. It cannot be undone, and your numbers cannot be moved to another provider. Cancelling your plan does not delete your account.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Your rights">
    Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information AgentNode holds about you. You may also have the right to opt out of certain types of data processing.

    To submit a privacy request, email [legal@goagentnode.com](mailto:legal@goagentnode.com) with the subject line **"Privacy Request"** and include enough detail to identify the information at issue. AgentNode will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Terms of service highlights

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Subscription and credits">
    Your plan is monthly or yearly, whichever you chose when you signed up, and renews automatically for the same length unless you cancel. Cancel with **Cancel plan** in **Settings › Billing**, or by email to [legal@goagentnode.com](mailto:legal@goagentnode.com), by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the day before the last day of your current period. Your plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

    Your plan includes credits, which texts, calls and other billable actions use. Plan credits are used first, expire at the end of each period, and do not roll over. Credits you buy on top do not expire while your subscription is active; any left when it ends, or when you delete your account, are lost. Credits have no cash value and are not refundable unless the Terms or the law require it.

    What each text, call and lookup costs is on the [Rates](https://goagentnode.com/rates) page. AgentNode may change subscription pricing or credit rates, but only after giving you at least 30 days' written notice before the renewal date on which the change takes effect.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Permitted use">
    AgentNode is for licensed real estate professionals conducting legitimate lead follow-up in connection with their real estate business. You may only use the platform for your own real estate activity — not on behalf of unrelated businesses, and not to resell or white-label access to others.

    Prohibited uses include contacting purchased or scraped lead lists, sending spam or deceptive messages, using the platform for debt collection or other restricted content categories, and any conduct that violates fair housing laws, consumer protection regulations, or carrier and registry requirements.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Messaging compliance obligations">
    You are the sender of every message AgentNode transmits on your behalf. You are solely responsible for ensuring your contact list has appropriate consent, that your business is registered correctly for texting, and that your outreach complies with the TCPA and applicable state laws.

    AgentNode provides tools to support compliance — including automatic opt-out processing, quiet-hour settings, and consent recording at import — but these are convenience tools. They do not constitute legal advice and do not guarantee that your messaging practices are legally sufficient. You remain responsible for your compliance program.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Calls and call recording">
    Calls you make through AgentNode are recorded and transcribed by default. Before recording begins, you are responsible for telling everyone on the call and getting their consent wherever the law requires it. Rules differ by state.

    If someone does not agree, the call must not be recorded or transcribed: end it, and reach them another way that is not recorded, such as your own phone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Human escalation requirement">
    AgentNode is designed to hand off conversations to you when a human response is needed. You are required to be available to handle those escalated conversations in a timely manner, particularly for complaints, consent questions, opt-out requests, fair-housing-sensitive topics, and any matter involving offers, transaction terms, or regulated real estate communications.

    AgentNode assists you — it does not replace your professional judgment or your obligations as a licensed real estate professional. Provider does not act as your broker, agent, fiduciary, or legal adviser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dispute resolution">
    Disputes between you and Whiteworth Inc. are governed by the law of the State of Delaware. Before commencing any formal proceeding, both parties agree to attempt good-faith resolution for 60 days after written notice of the dispute.

    Disputes that cannot be resolved informally are submitted to binding individual arbitration, administered by the American Arbitration Association (AAA) under its Commercial Arbitration Rules, seated in Delaware. Class actions, collective actions, and representative proceedings are waived to the maximum extent permitted by applicable law — claims may only be brought in an individual capacity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
