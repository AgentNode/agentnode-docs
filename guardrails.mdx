title: "Guardrails: When AgentNode Stops and Hands Off to You"
sidebarTitle: "Guardrails"
description: "AgentNode's guardrails ensure an assistant only answers when it's confident, and brings every other conversation to you in the Inbox."
---

Guardrails are the boundaries that tell an assistant when to keep going and when to stop. Whenever a conversation moves into territory that needs a licensed professional, a clear human decision, or anything the assistant is not confident about, it stops replying and hands the conversation to you before it can say the wrong thing. You stay in control of every conversation that matters most.

## What triggers a hand-off

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sensitive topic" icon="shield">
    The lead raises a subject that needs a licensed professional's answer, such as price, offers and contracts, commission, legal questions, or property condition.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not sure enough" icon="circle-question">
    The lead asks something the assistant cannot answer with confidence, from the conversation, the lead's record and what you have told it. Rather than guess, it brings the question to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An upset lead" icon="face-frown">
    The lead is angry, complaining, or confused by the last text. A person answers them next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suspects a machine" icon="robot">
    The lead asks whether this is a bot, or tests it with something only a person could answer. They hear from you next, never from AgentNode.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How escalation works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The assistant stops replying">
    AgentNode pauses texting that lead. Nothing more is sent to them from AgentNode while it waits for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The conversation lands in your Inbox">
    It appears in the **Inbox** on your home screen, from the assistant that stopped.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You see the full context">
    The lead's history, what they asked, and why the assistant stopped are all there, so you can jump in without catching up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You respond directly">
    Press **Let me answer** in the Inbox, or reply from the conversation. When you are done, press **Carry on** and the assistant picks up from where it stopped. Or **Keep it paused**, if you would rather handle that lead yourself.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Consent and escalation at import

AgentNode only contacts leads you have brought in. When you import a CSV or switch on an auto-import, you confirm that these are people you already do business with, or who have agreed to hear from you. AgentNode keeps that record on your account. If a lead has no such record, AgentNode asks you before texting them.

<Note>
  AgentNode errs on the side of caution. If an assistant is not confident, it stops and asks you. It never guesses at sensitive real estate questions or legal matters.
</Note>
