---

Guardrails are the boundaries that tell an assistant when to keep going and when to stop. Whenever a conversation moves into territory that needs a licensed professional, a clear human decision, or anything the assistant is not confident about, it stops replying and hands the thread to you before it can say the wrong thing. You stay in control of every conversation that matters most.

## What triggers a hand-off

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sensitive topic" icon="shield">
    The lead raises a subject that needs a licensed professional's answer, such as price, offers and contracts, commission, legal questions, or property condition.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Not sure enough" icon="circle-question">
    The lead asks something the assistant cannot answer with confidence, from the thread, the lead's record and what you have told it. Rather than guess, it brings the question to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An upset lead" icon="face-frown">
    The lead is angry, complaining, or confused by the last text. A person answers them next.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Suspects a machine" icon="robot">
    The lead asks whether this is a bot, or tests it with something nobody would ask an agent. They hear from you next, never from the next automated line.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Each job also has its own **Guardrails** tab: the rules it keeps, such as never quoting a price or never promising a time, each with a switch. See [Your assistants](/assistants#guardrails).

## How escalation works

<Steps>
  <Step title="The assistant stops replying">
    Automatic replies stop on that thread. Nothing more is sent to that person while it waits for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The thread lands in your Inbox">
    It appears in the **Inbox** on your home screen, from the assistant that stopped, and under **Needs your attention** on that assistant's Overview.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You see the full context">
    The lead's history, what they asked, and why the assistant stopped are all there, so you can jump in without catching up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You respond directly">
    Reply from the thread. When you are done, hand the thread back and the assistant resumes from where it left off, or keep it yourself if you would rather stay in the driving seat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Consent and escalation at import

AgentNode only contacts leads you have brought in. When you import a CSV or connect a flow, you confirm that the contacts are people you have a legitimate prior business relationship with, or who have otherwise given consent to be contacted. That record is tied to your account from the moment they arrive, and a lead without it is a question to you, never a text.

<Note>
  AgentNode errs on the side of caution. If an assistant is not confident, it stops and asks you. It never guesses at sensitive real estate questions or legal matters.
</Note>
