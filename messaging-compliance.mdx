SMS marketing in real estate is regulated at the federal level by the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), and state laws may impose additional requirements. AgentNode is built with compliance in mind — it automatically processes opt-outs, records consent at import, and enforces the sending schedule you configure. Even so, you as the AgentNode customer are the sender of every message and are legally responsible for how you use the platform. Understanding your obligations is a core part of using AgentNode responsibly.

## Your responsibilities

As the sender of every message AgentNode transmits on your behalf, you are responsible for the following:

## Consent at import

When you import a CSV into AgentNode, you are asked to confirm that your contact list has the appropriate consent for SMS outreach before the import is finalised. AgentNode records the time and source of that confirmation as your consent attestation for the batch.

This confirmation is not a formality. By importing a list, you are representing that every contact on it has consented to receive texts from you — or that you have a prior business relationship that provides a lawful basis for contact. If that is not true for every contact, do not import them.

## Opt-out handling

When a lead replies **STOP** to any message sent through AgentNode, the platform processes the opt-out immediately and permanently. That contact is suppressed across every assistant and job. AgentNode will not send them another message.

Opt-outs cannot be overridden or reversed unless the contact subsequently provides fresh, documented consent. AgentNode will not re-enrol an opted-out contact automatically. If a lead genuinely re-engages and consents again, you will need to add them back with the appropriate consent documented.

## Calling restrictions

AgentNode is an SMS and text messaging platform. If calling features are available on your account, they are subject to separate compliance rules including federal and state telemarketing laws, do-not-call requirements, and calling-hour restrictions.

If you use call recording features, you are responsible for disclosing the recording to all parties before it begins. Recording-consent requirements vary significantly by state — some require only one party to consent, others require all parties. You are solely responsible for determining what applies in your state and ensuring the required disclosures are made on every recorded call.

## Real estate-specific restrictions

AgentNode is designed for licensed real estate professionals conducting legitimate lead follow-up in connection with their real estate business. A few additional restrictions apply specifically to real estate use:

- **Use within your licensed activity** — do not use AgentNode for purposes outside your licensed real estate activity. Using it to contact leads on behalf of unrelated businesses, or to send messages unconnected to your real estate practice, is not a permitted use.
- **Fair housing compliance** — do not use AgentNode in any way that could constitute steering, discrimination, or exclusion on the basis of a protected class. This includes targeting, routing, or messaging decisions that could violate fair housing laws. You are solely responsible for ensuring your assistants' jobs and contact practices comply with the Fair Housing Act and applicable state equivalents.
