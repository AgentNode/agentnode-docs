AgentNode is a team of AI assistants built for real estate agents. Three assistants text your leads from your number, in your voice: **Lead Nurture** reaches every new lead and follows up until they talk or book, **Client Relations** keeps past clients close with the texts you never find time for, and **Appointments** books the call when a lead is ready and rescues the meetings that slip. Each one answers replies on its own and hands a thread to you the moment it needs a human touch, so the contacts you already have turn into booked appointments without adding hours to your day.

## The problem it solves

Most agents have thousands of contacts sitting in a CRM or spreadsheet that never get a proper follow-up. New leads arrive while you are at a showing, past clients hear from you once a year, and a missed appointment quietly becomes a lost deal. Those contacts are real opportunities that go cold because there was never enough time.

AgentNode gives each of those situations an assistant. It does not send blasts: every text is written for one person, from what you know about them and what they have said, and every reply is answered or brought to you.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Import a CSV, add people by hand, or connect the places your leads come from under **Contact flows**: your website form, Zillow, Follow Up Boss, an open house sign-in sheet. Each flow tags the people it brings in and says which assistant looks after them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Each lead gets one owner">
    Every lead is looked after by exactly one assistant, or by you. AgentNode picks the owner from where the lead came from and what you have told it, and you can change it on the lead's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assistant runs its jobs">
    A new lead gets a first text within minutes, a reply gets an answer, a quiet lead gets a follow-up, a birthday gets a text on the day. Every text passes through the [weave](/ai-messaging#the-weave), which fits it into the conversation so far so your texts read as one person texting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You answer what needs you">
    Questions, decisions and threads that need a person land in the **Inbox** on your home screen, from the assistant that asked. You answer there, or talk to any assistant on its own page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Three assistants" icon="users">
    Lead Nurture, Client Relations and Appointments, each with a set of jobs you can switch on and off and tell how you like them done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Texts in your voice" icon="message-lines">
    Each text is written to one person, from your templates and what you know about them, and woven into the conversation so nothing repeats or reads like a machine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Price, contracts, legal questions and anything unclear come straight to you instead of being answered wrong.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-out management" icon="ban">
    Any contact who replies STOP is removed from every assistant immediately, and their status is permanently logged.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where things are

| Section | What you'll find there |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Your home screen: ask or tell AgentNode anything, and the **Inbox** of what needs you. |
| **Contacts** | Everyone you have brought in, their tags and owner, and **Contact flows**, the places leads come from. |
| **Conversations** | Every thread, most recent first, with a reply box. |
| **Bookings** | The appointments your leads have booked. |
| **Your assistants** | Lead Nurture, Client Relations and Appointments: what each is doing, its jobs, and a chat with it. |
| **Settings** | Your account, your number, and the rules every text follows. |
