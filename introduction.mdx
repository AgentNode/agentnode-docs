AgentNode is an AI-powered SMS follow-up platform built for real estate agents. It automatically texts your dormant leads with personalised messages, keeps those conversations going on its own, and hands off to you the moment a reply needs a human touch — turning the contacts you already have into booked appointments without adding hours to your day.

## The problem it solves

Most agents have thousands of contacts sitting in a CRM or spreadsheet that have never received a proper follow-up. Life gets busy, lists grow stale, and writing a personalised message to every person feels impossible at scale. Those contacts represent real opportunities — buyers and sellers who once expressed interest — but they go cold simply because there wasn't enough time to reach out.

AgentNode works through that entire list automatically. It doesn't send generic blasts; it drafts an individual message for every contact based on what you know about them, sends it in your voice, and manages the follow-up thread until the conversation is ready for you.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import your contacts from a CSV">
    Export your existing database from any source — your CRM, a spreadsheet, or wherever you keep contacts — and upload the file. AgentNode reads names, phone numbers, and any notes you've included against each contact.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI drafts a personalised message for each lead">
    AgentNode writes an individual follow-up text for every contact on your list, drawing on what you know about them and your configured messaging preferences. Each message is written to that specific person, not copied from a template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review, send, and book the replies">
    Approve or edit the drafts, then send. AgentNode handles ongoing replies automatically, escalating to you the moment a conversation needs a decision only you can make. You show up, close the appointment, and move on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Database Reactivation" icon="database">
    Every dormant contact in your database gets surfaced and worked — not just the ones you happened to remember this week.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AI-Drafted Messaging" icon="message-lines">
    Each message is written to that specific person, referencing what you know about them and conditions relevant to their area.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Smart Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    AgentNode answers what it can and escalates the rest — price, contracts, and anything unclear come straight to your queue instead of being answered incorrectly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-Out Management" icon="ban">
    Any contact who replies to stop is removed from active outreach immediately, and their status is permanently logged so they never surface again in future campaigns.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
