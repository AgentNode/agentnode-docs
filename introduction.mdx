AgentNode is a team of AI assistants for real estate agents. They text your leads from your number, in your voice, answer what comes back, and book the appointment. When a conversation needs a real person, they stop and bring it to you.

There are three of them, and each has a job:

- **Lead Nurture** texts every new lead within minutes, follows up until they talk, and books the call.
- **Client Relations** keeps past clients close: birthdays, home anniversaries, and asking for reviews and referrals.
- **Appointment Recovery** chases anyone who missed a meeting or has not confirmed one, and gets it rebooked.

## The problem it solves

Most agents have hundreds or thousands of contacts sitting in a CRM or a spreadsheet that never get a proper follow-up. New leads arrive while you are at a showing, past clients hear from you once a year, and a missed appointment quietly becomes a lost deal. There was never enough time.

AgentNode gives each of those situations an assistant. It does not send blasts. Every text is written for one person, from what you know about them and what they have said, and every reply is either answered or brought to you.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Connect Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel, upload a spreadsheet, or add people one at a time. You confirm they agreed to hear from you by text, and AgentNode keeps that on file. Nobody is texted without it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AgentNode looks after them">
    Everyone you bring in is **managed**: the assistants text them and answer for you. Whatever happens with a lead, such as a reply, a meeting or a long silence, goes to the assistant whose job it is. Switch **Managed** off on anyone's page and AgentNode leaves them alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The assistants do their jobs">
    A new lead gets a first text within minutes. A reply gets an answer. A quiet lead gets a follow-up. A past client gets a text on their birthday. Each text is written to fit the conversation so far, so it reads as one person texting, never a script.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You answer what needs you">
    Questions, decisions and conversations that need a person land in the **Inbox** on your home screen, from the assistant that asked. You answer there, or tell AgentNode what you want done.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What makes it safe to switch on

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your number, your voice" icon="message-lines">
    Texts go from the number you picked and are written as you, from the facts you give and the conversation so far. Nothing reads like a machine.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Price, contracts, legal questions, upset leads and anything the assistant is unsure of come straight to you instead of being answered wrong.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Consent and opt-outs" icon="ban">
    Nobody is texted without agreement on file. Anyone who replies STOP is opted out at once, for every assistant, permanently.
  </Card>
  <Card title="You decide how hands-on to be" icon="hand">
    Have every first text wait for your approval, pick how sure a reply must be before it sends on its own, or take over any conversation yourself.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Where things are

The menu on the left of every screen has these:

| Section | What you'll find there |
|---|---|
| **Operator** | Your home screen: ask or tell AgentNode anything, and the **Inbox** of what needs you. |
| **Contacts** | Everyone you have brought in, their tags, and **Auto-import**, the sources that bring leads in on their own. |
| **Conversations** | Every text thread, most recent first, with a reply box. |
| **Bookings** | The appointments your leads have booked. |
| **Your assistants** | Lead Nurture, Client Relations and Appointment Recovery: how each is doing, and its jobs. |
| **Settings** | Your account, your business registration, your number, and the rules every text follows. |
| **Book onboarding call** | A calendar to pick a time, and a person walks you through setup. |
| **Docs** | These pages. |

On every screen there is also an **AgentNode** button at the bottom right. Press it to open the same chat as Operator beside whatever you are looking at.
