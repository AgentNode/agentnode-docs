Welcome to the AgentNode documentation. AgentNode is a team of AI assistants for working real estate agents: **Lead Nurture** texts every new lead and follows up until they talk or book, **Client Relations** keeps your past clients close, and **Appointments** books calls and saves the meetings that slip. Every text goes from your number, in your voice, and anything that needs a person comes to you in the Inbox. These docs walk you through setting up your account, bringing in contacts, what each assistant does, and the guardrails that keep your outreach compliant and on-brand.

## How AgentNode works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and set up your number">
    Create your account, confirm your email, and set up the number your texts go from. **Settings › Messaging** holds the rules every text follows: quiet hours, the opt-out line, and whether the first text to a new contact waits for your approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Import a CSV, add people one at a time, or connect the places your leads come from under **Contact flows**: your website form, Zillow, Follow Up Boss, an open house sign-in sheet. Each flow tags the people it brings in and says which assistant looks after them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your assistants start working">
    Every lead has one owner. Lead Nurture texts a new lead within minutes, learns what they are looking for and follows up when they go quiet. Client Relations sends birthday and anniversary texts and asks for reviews and referrals. Appointments books the call when a lead is ready and chases anyone who missed or has not confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You answer what needs you">
    When an assistant needs a fact, a decision, or a person on the thread, it asks in the **Inbox** on your home screen. One answer serves everyone waiting on it. Anything sensitive, such as price, contracts or an upset lead, is handed to you rather than answered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Three assistants" icon="users">
    Each assistant runs a set of jobs: New lead, Follow-up and Re-engagement for Lead Nurture; Birthdays and anniversaries, Review request and Referral ask for Client Relations; Book a call, Missed appointment and Unconfirmed booking for Appointments. Switch a job on or off, and tell it how you like it done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-reply" icon="robot">
    Every text a lead sends is answered, whoever looks after them, as long as AgentNode is sure enough of the answer. It books the time they agree to, and hands the thread to you when it is not sure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Built-in guardrails stop an assistant answering what it should not, such as price, contracts and legal questions, and bring the thread to you instead. Each job has its own guardrails you can switch on and off.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-out management" icon="ban">
    Any contact who replies STOP is opted out straight away. AgentNode never texts them again, and no assistant can pick them up. Their status is permanently logged.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
