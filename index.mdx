Welcome to the AgentNode docs. AgentNode is a team of AI assistants for working real estate agents. **Lead Nurture** texts every new lead and books the call, **Client Relations** keeps your past clients close, and **Appointment Recovery** saves the meetings that slip. Every text goes from your number, in your voice. Anything that needs a person comes to you.

These pages show you how to set up, bring in your contacts, and what each assistant does. No technical skills are needed.

## How AgentNode works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your account">
    Create your account, pay for your plan, give AgentNode your business details so it can register you for texting, and pick the number your texts go from. The [Quickstart](/quickstart) walks you through it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Connect Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel, upload a spreadsheet, or add people one at a time. You confirm they agreed to hear from you; AgentNode never texts anyone without that.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your assistants start working">
    Lead Nurture texts a new lead within minutes, answers what they text back, books the call when they are ready, and follows up when they go quiet. Client Relations sends birthday and anniversary texts and asks for reviews and referrals. Appointment Recovery chases anyone who missed a meeting or has not confirmed one. They check their plans against each other, so a lead only ever hears from one person: you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You answer what needs you">
    When an assistant needs a fact, a decision, or you to step in, it asks in the **Inbox** on your home screen. Anything sensitive, such as price, contracts or an upset lead, is handed to you rather than answered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Three assistants" icon="users">
    Each assistant has a set of jobs. Switch any job on or off, and tell it how you like things done, in plain words.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Automatic replies" icon="robot">
    Every text a lead sends is answered, after the few minutes a person would take, as long as AgentNode is sure of the answer. When it is not, the conversation comes to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Price, contracts, legal questions and upset leads are never answered by an assistant. They come straight to you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-outs handled" icon="ban">
    Anyone who replies STOP is opted out at once and never texted again, by any assistant.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  These docs cover the AgentNode web app and the iPhone app. Everything happens in your browser or on your phone. There is nothing to install on a computer and no coding involved.
</Note>
