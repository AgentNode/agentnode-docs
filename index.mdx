Welcome to the AgentNode documentation. AgentNode is an AI-powered SMS follow-up platform built for working real estate agents — it reaches out to every dormant lead in your database with a personalised text, keeps the conversation going on its own, and hands control back to you the moment a reply needs a human touch. These docs walk you through everything: setting up your account, importing contacts, running campaigns, managing your inbox, and understanding the guardrails that keep your outreach compliant and on-brand.

## How AgentNode works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and configure your preferences">
    Create your account and tell AgentNode two things: what you message leads about and when you want messages sent. You can update both at any time in **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your contacts">
    Export your existing database from your CRM or spreadsheet as a CSV and upload it from the Inbox. AgentNode reads names, phone numbers, and any notes you include. At import you confirm that these are contacts you have a prior relationship with — that record is stored on file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI sends personalised texts in your voice">
    AgentNode drafts an individual follow-up message for every lead, drawing on what you know about them and current market conditions in their area. Messages go out on your configured schedule, and AgentNode handles replies automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You take over when it matters">
    Whenever a conversation reaches a question AgentNode can't answer — pricing, contracts, or anything unclear — it stops, flags the reason, and surfaces the thread at the top of your **Needs You** queue. You pick up the conversation exactly where it left off.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="AI Messaging" icon="robot">
    AgentNode writes a personalised follow-up for each lead in your voice, so every contact gets a message that feels like it came from you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Built-in guardrails detect questions AgentNode shouldn't answer and escalate those conversations to you instantly, keeping you in control of every sensitive topic.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-Out Management" icon="ban">
    Any contact who replies STOP is removed from your active queue immediately, and their status is permanently logged so they're never messaged again.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
