Welcome to the AgentNode documentation. AgentNode is a team of AI assistants for working real estate agents: **Lead Nurture** texts every new lead and follows up until they talk or book, **Client Relations** keeps your past clients close, and **Appointment Recovery** books calls and saves the meetings that slip. Every text goes from your number, in your voice, and anything that needs a person comes to you in the Inbox. These docs walk you through setting up your account, bringing in contacts, what each assistant does, and the guardrails that keep your outreach compliant and on-brand.

## How AgentNode works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up and set up your number">
    Create your account, confirm your email, and set up the number your texts go from. **Settings › Messaging** holds the rules every text follows: quiet hours, the opt-out line, and whether the first text to a new contact waits for your approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Import a CSV, add people one at a time, or switch on **Auto-import** for Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel or a Slack channel, so new leads come in on their own. Each source tags the people it brings in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your assistants start working">
    Everyone you bring in is managed, and whatever happens with them goes to every assistant it concerns. Lead Nurture texts a new lead within minutes, learns what they are looking for and follows up when they go quiet. Client Relations sends birthday and anniversary texts and asks for reviews and referrals. Appointment Recovery books the call when a lead is ready and chases anyone who missed or has not confirmed. AgentNode checks their plans against each other, so a lead hears from one person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You answer what needs you">
    When an assistant needs a fact, a decision, or a person on the thread, it asks in the **Inbox** on your home screen. One answer serves everyone waiting on it. Anything sensitive, such as price, contracts or an upset lead, is handed to you rather than answered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What's included

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Three assistants" icon="users">
    Each assistant runs a set of jobs: New lead, Auto-reply, Follow-up and Re-engagement for Lead Nurture; Birthdays and anniversaries, Review request and Referral ask for Client Relations; Book a call, Missed appointment and Unconfirmed booking for Appointment Recovery. Switch a job on or off, and tell it how you like it done.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Auto-reply" icon="robot">
    Every text a managed lead sends is answered, as long as AgentNode is sure enough of the answer, after the few minutes a person would take. It books the time they agree to, and hands the thread to you when it is not sure.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Guardrails" icon="shield-check">
    Built-in guardrails stop an assistant answering what it should not, such as price, contracts and legal questions, and bring the thread to you instead.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Opt-out management" icon="ban">
    Any contact who replies STOP is opted out straight away. AgentNode never texts them again, and no assistant can pick them up. Their status is permanently logged.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
