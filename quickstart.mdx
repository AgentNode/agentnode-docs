Setup takes about ten minutes, in two parts. First the admin: your plan, your business details for the phone companies, and your number. Then the welcome: what AgentNode knows about you, your calendar and your contacts. By the end, AgentNode's assistants are looking after everyone you brought in.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [goagentnode.com](https://www.goagentnode.com) and click **Get early access**. Enter your full name and work email, and AgentNode emails you a code to confirm the address. If you would rather sign in with a password, press **Set a password instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your plan">
    Choose a plan and press **Pay and continue**. Payment happens on Stripe, which brings you back to the next step. Your plan includes credits for the texts, calls and AI that AgentNode uses, and you can cancel any time from **Settings › Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    US phone companies (the carriers, like AT&T, Verizon and T-Mobile) make every business register before it texts customers, so its texts are not blocked as spam. AgentNode fills in and sends the registration for you. It needs your details exactly as the IRS has them.

    - Choose **I have a registered business** or **I'm a sole proprietor**.
    - Enter your EIN (your business's tax ID number from the IRS), your legal business name exactly as it appears on the IRS letter that gave you the EIN (called CP 575), the business address the IRS has for it, your business phone and a business email.
    - Optionally, add the name your leads know you by and your website. AgentNode reads your website to learn about your business.

    No EIN yet? Press **Don't have an EIN yet?**. You can apply free on the IRS website in about ten minutes and get it on the spot. Not ready? Press **Finish later**, and you start on this step next time you sign in. Approval takes 1–3 business days, and you can keep going and start texting in the meantime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your number">
    Enter an area code, then press **Use** next to the number you want. You are not charged for a number until you do. This is the number your leads see, text and call, and it stays yours.

    You can text from it straight away. Until your own registration is approved, your texts go out under AgentNode's approval. Once yours is approved, AgentNode switches you over by itself, and your leads never see a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Welcome

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what AgentNode knows about you">
    AgentNode reads your website for the facts leads ask about, like your brokerage, your areas and your languages. If you gave your website with your business details, the facts are already there. Remove any that are wrong. Otherwise, enter your website and press **Read my website**, or **Skip for now**.

    Your assistants use these facts when a lead asks about them, and never bring them up on their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Press **Connect Google Calendar** so AgentNode only offers times you are free, and every meeting it books lands on your calendar. Only your busy times are shared, never what your events are. You can **Skip for now** and connect it later under **Settings › Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Connect your CRM, drop in a CSV, or add people one at a time.

    To bring people in from **Follow Up Boss**, press **Connect**, then **Sign in to Follow Up Boss** and press **Allow**. Choose who comes in, confirm they have agreed to hear from you by text, then press **Import**.

    For a CSV:

    1. Check what each column in your file holds, such as name, phone and email. Name and phone are required.
    2. Confirm the people in the file have agreed to hear from you. AgentNode never texts anyone without consent on file.
    3. Press **Import contacts**. AgentNode removes duplicates, so you can upload an updated copy of the same list without creating double entries.

    You need at least one contact to continue. You can always add more from **Contacts**, and **Contacts › Auto-import** brings new leads in on their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your assistants take it from here">
    The last screen confirms that AgentNode's assistants are looking after your contacts, including anyone you add later. All three assistants start working straight away, doing everything they can. You can switch any of their jobs off later. Press **Go to your overview** to open [Operator](/operator), or **Go to your contacts**.

    While the phone companies review your business, your texts go out under AgentNode's approval. **Settings › Business** shows where your registration stands, and what to fix if it is turned down.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

**Lead Nurture** texts each new lead within minutes, answers what they text back, and follows up while they are quiet. **Client Relations** keeps past clients close, and **Appointment Recovery** rebooks the meetings that slip. Every text goes from your number, inside your hours, and only to people with consent on file.

When something needs you, such as a question about price or contracts or a lead who wants a person, it lands in the **Inbox** on Operator. Everything else keeps running.

To make the texts sound more like you, fill in **Settings › Realtor profile** and check the rules in **Settings › Messaging**, like approving the first text to each new contact. For a walkthrough with the team, press **Book onboarding call** in the menu on the left.
