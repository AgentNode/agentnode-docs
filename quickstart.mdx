Setup takes about ten minutes, in two parts. First the admin: your plan, your business details for the carriers, and your number. Then the welcome: what AgentNode knows about you, your calendar and your contacts. By the end, AgentNode's assistants are looking after everyone you brought in.

## Set up your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [goagentnode.com](https://www.goagentnode.com) and click **Get early access**. Enter your full name and work email, and AgentNode emails you a code to confirm the address. If you would rather sign in with a password, press **Set a password instead**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your plan">
    Choose a plan and press **Pay and continue**. Payment happens on Stripe, which brings you back to the next step. Your plan includes credits for the texts, calls and AI that AgentNode uses, and you can cancel any time from **Settings › Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    US carriers require every business that sends texts to register first. AgentNode files the registration for you; it needs your details exactly as the IRS has them.

    - Choose **I have a registered business** or **I'm a sole proprietor**.
    - Enter the legal name as it appears on your EIN confirmation letter (CP 575), your EIN, the business address registered for it, your business phone and a business email.
    - Optionally, add the name your leads know you by and your website. AgentNode reads your website to learn about your business.

    No EIN yet? Press **Don't have an EIN yet?**. You can apply free on the IRS website in about ten minutes and get it on the spot. Press **Finish later**, and when you sign in again you land back on this step. Carrier approval takes 1–3 business days, and you do not wait for it to carry on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your number">
    Enter an area code, pick one of the numbers suggested in it, and press **Use** with your number to confirm it. Nothing is bought until you pick. This is the number your leads see, text and call, and it stays yours.

    You can text from it straight away, on AgentNode's own approved campaign, while the carriers approve yours. Once they do, it moves to your campaign on its own and your leads never see it change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Welcome

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what AgentNode knows about you">
    AgentNode's research assistant reads your website for the facts leads ask about, like your brokerage, your areas and your languages. If you gave your website with your business details, the facts are already there. Remove any that are wrong. Otherwise, enter your website and press **Read my website**, or **Skip for now**.

    Your assistants quote these facts when a lead asks, and never volunteer them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Press **Connect Google Calendar** so AgentNode only offers times you are free, and every meeting it books lands on your calendar. Only your busy times are shared, never what your events are. You can **Skip for now** and connect it later under **Settings › Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Connect your CRM, drop in a CSV, or add people one at a time.

    To bring everyone in from **Follow Up Boss** or **GoHighLevel**, press **Connect** and paste your API key (for GoHighLevel, a private integration token and the Location ID). Confirm everyone in it has agreed to hear from you by text, then press **Import everyone**.

    For a CSV:

    1. Check which column is which. Name and phone are required.
    2. Confirm the people in the file have agreed to hear from you. AgentNode never texts anyone without consent on file.
    3. Press **Import contacts**. AgentNode removes duplicates, so you can upload an updated copy of the same list without creating double entries.

    You need at least one contact to continue. You can always add more from **Contacts**, and **Contacts › Auto-import** brings new leads in on their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your assistants take it from here">
    The last screen confirms that AgentNode's assistants are looking after your contacts, including anyone you add later. Every job of all three assistants is on from the start. Press **Go to your overview** to open [Operator](/operator), or **Go to your contacts**.

    While the carriers review your business, your number texts on AgentNode's campaign. **Settings › Business** shows where your registration stands, and what to correct if the carriers send it back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

**Lead Nurture** texts each new lead within minutes, answers what they text back, and follows up while they are quiet. **Client Relations** keeps past clients close, and **Appointment Recovery** rebooks the meetings that slip. Every text goes from your number, inside your hours, and only to people with consent on file.

When something needs you, such as a question about price or contracts or a lead who wants a person, it lands in the **Inbox** on Operator. Everything else keeps running.

To make the texts sound more like you, fill in **Settings › Realtor profile** and check the rules in **Settings › Messaging**, like approving the first text to each new contact. For a walkthrough with the team, press **Book onboarding call** in the left nav.
