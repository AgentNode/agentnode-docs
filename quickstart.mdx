This guide takes you from a blank account to assistants working your leads. By the end you will have your number set up, your contacts in, and Lead Nurture texting new leads on your behalf, all in a single session.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [goagentnode.com](https://www.goagentnode.com) and click **Get early access**. Enter your name and work email address, then check your inbox for the confirmation code AgentNode sends you. Enter the code to verify your email. You can set a password, or sign in with an email code every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your messaging">
    Open **Settings › Messaging**. Your texts go from your own number, so set that up first. Then check the rules every text follows:

    - **Always approve the first text to a new contact**: the opener waits in your Inbox until you approve it. Follow-ups send as usual.
    - **Quiet hours**: nothing sends overnight; a text due then waits for morning.
    - **Opt-out line on first text**: the first text carries "Reply STOP to opt out".
    - **Send on weekends**: off unless you want weekend texts.

    Fill in **Settings › Realtor profile** too. It is how the assistants know who you are and how you sound.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Open **Contacts** and either press **Import from CSV** or open the **Auto-import** tab and switch on the places your leads come from. To add one person, tell [Operator](/operator). For a CSV:

    1. Upload your file and map the columns to AgentNode's fields (name, number, notes, and any custom data).
    2. AgentNode remembers your column mapping, so future imports from the same source are instant.
    3. Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. That record is stored on file.

    AgentNode deduplicates your contacts automatically, so uploading an updated version of the same list will not create double entries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Let your assistants start">
    Open **Your assistants** in the left nav. Each assistant's page shows how it is doing and the jobs it runs, and each job takes your working preferences: how you like it done, in your words. Lead Nurture's **New lead** job is on by default: every new lead with consent on file gets a first text within minutes, in your hours.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Once the first texts are out, the assistants take over the follow-up. Auto-reply answers what leads text back, Lead Nurture follows up with anyone who goes quiet, and Appointment Recovery offers times from your calendar when someone is ready to talk. You do not need to be involved in every exchange.

When something needs you, such as a fact an assistant does not have, a question about price or contracts, or a lead who wants a person, it lands in the **Inbox** on your home screen, from the assistant that asked. That is your signal to step in. Everything else keeps running while you focus on the conversations that matter most.
