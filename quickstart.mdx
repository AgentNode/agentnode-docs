Setting up takes about 15 minutes, in two parts. First the admin: your plan, your business details and your number. Then the welcome: what AgentNode knows about you, your calendar and your contacts. When you finish, AgentNode's assistants are looking after everyone you brought in.

## Before you start

Have these to hand:

- **A card** to pay for your plan. Payment is taken by Stripe, which handles payments for many of the sites you already use. AgentNode never sees your card number.
- **Your business's EIN.** This is the nine-digit tax ID the IRS gave your business, written like 12-3456789. It is on the letter the IRS sent when you got it, called a CP 575. Sole proprietors need one too. No EIN yet? Step 3 shows how to get one free in about ten minutes.
- **Your website address**, if you have one. AgentNode reads it to learn about your business.
- **Your contacts.** Either your login for Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel, or a spreadsheet of names and mobile numbers from wherever you keep them.

<Note>
  Everything happens in your web browser. There is nothing to install, and nothing technical to do.
</Note>

## Part 1: Your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [goagentnode.com](https://www.goagentnode.com) and press **Get started**.

    Enter your full name and work email, tick the box to agree to the terms, and press **Create account**. AgentNode emails you a code. Type it in and press **Continue**.

    That code is also how you sign in from now on, so there is no password to remember. If you would rather have one, press **Set a password instead** before you create the account.

    AgentNode also emails you a welcome note with a link to this guide and one to book a call with the team, for whenever you would like a hand.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay for your plan">
    The screen shows the plan and what it costs. Press **Pay and continue**. Stripe's secure payment page opens; enter your card, and you come straight back to AgentNode. It may say **Confirming your plan** for a few seconds while Stripe confirms the payment.

    Your plan includes credits. Credits pay for the texts, calls and AI that AgentNode uses, and one credit is one dollar. You can cancel any time under **Settings › Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tell us about your business">
    US phone carriers require every business that sends texts to register first. AgentNode files that registration for you. It only needs your details exactly as the IRS has them, because the carriers check them against IRS records.

    1. Choose **I have a registered business** or **I'm a sole proprietor**.
    2. Enter the legal name exactly as it appears on your IRS letter, your EIN, and the address the IRS has for it.
    3. Add your business phone and email. The carriers write to that email if they have a question.
    4. If you have a website, enter it. AgentNode reads it to learn about you.
    5. Press **Continue**.

    **No EIN yet?** Press **Don't have an EIN yet?** under the EIN box. It links to the IRS website, where applying is free, takes about ten minutes, and gives you your EIN on the spot. Press **Finish later** if you need to leave; signing in again brings you straight back to this step.

    **Got your EIN in the last six months?** Tick **My EIN was issued in the last 180 days** and upload the IRS letter. The carriers ask to see it for new EINs.

    Carrier approval takes 1 to 3 business days. You do not wait for it: carry on, and you can text in the meantime.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your number">
    This is the number your leads see, text and call back, so choose an area code they know. Enter it, pick one of the numbers suggested, and press **Use**, which shows the number you picked. Nothing is bought until you press it, and the number stays yours. It costs a few credits from your plan, then a few credits a month; the screen shows the exact amounts.

    You can text from it straight away. While the carriers review your business, your texts go out under AgentNode's own approval. Once yours comes through, your number moves across by itself, and your leads never see a change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: The welcome

The welcome is three short questions. Press **Let's go** to start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check what AgentNode knows about you">
    If you gave your website, AgentNode has already read it and lists the facts it found: your brokerage, the areas you cover, the languages you speak. Remove any that are wrong with the **×** beside it. If you did not give a website, enter it and press **Read my website**, or press **Skip for now**.

    Your assistants quote these facts when a lead asks, and never bring them up on their own. You can add to them any time under **Settings › Realtor profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your calendar">
    Press **Connect** beside Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar or Apple Calendar, and sign in. AgentNode then only offers times you are free, and every meeting it books lands on your calendar. It only sees when you are busy, never what your events are.

    Apple Calendar asks for an app-specific password rather than your Apple ID password. See [Settings](/settings#your-calendar) for how to make one.

    You can **Skip for now** and connect it later under **Settings › Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring in your contacts">
    Pick whichever way suits you. You need at least one contact, or a CRM import switched on, to finish, and you can add more any time.

    **From Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel.** Press **Connect** on its row, then **Sign in to Follow Up Boss** (or GoHighLevel) and allow AgentNode. From then on, new people arrive in AgentNode as your CRM adds them. The people already in your CRM stay out unless you tick **Also bring in people already in Follow Up Boss**, and then you choose **Everyone already there** or **Only some**, by stage or tag. Most CRMs hold plenty of people who should not hear from you, so leave the box off or choose carefully. Switch on **Managed by AgentNode** for AgentNode to start on them; it starts off. Tick the box confirming they agreed to hear from you by text, then press the import button: **Bring in new people from** your CRM, **Import everyone from** it, or **Import the people chosen**.

    **From a spreadsheet.** AgentNode takes a CSV file. That is a plain spreadsheet file, and every CRM can export one, as can Google Sheets (**File › Download › Comma Separated Values**) and Excel (**Save As**, then choose CSV). Press **Upload**, then **Choose a file**. AgentNode works out which column is which; check that name and phone are right. Tick the box confirming these people agreed to hear from you, and tick **Managed by AgentNode** for AgentNode to start on them. Then press **Import**; the button says how many contacts it will bring in. Anyone already in AgentNode is skipped, so uploading the same list twice does not create doubles.

    **One at a time.** Press **Add**, enter a name and mobile number, tick that they agreed to hear from you, tick **Managed by AgentNode** for AgentNode to start on them, and press **Add contact**.

    AgentNode never texts anyone without that agreement on file. When your contacts are in, press **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Done">
    The last screen says **AgentNode is now managing your contacts**. Press **Go to your overview** to open [Operator](/operator), your home screen, or **Go to your contacts**.

    It also says where your carrier registration stands. **Settings › Business** shows the same at any time, with what to correct if the carriers send anything back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens next

Your assistants start on everyone you switched to **Managed by AgentNode**: from your number, in your voice, inside your hours, and only to people who agreed to hear from you. People you import are treated as contacts you already know, not brand-new leads, so most get one short, low-pressure check-in rather than an introduction. New leads who arrive later get a first text within minutes.

When something needs you, such as a question about price or a lead who wants to talk to a person, it lands in the **Inbox** on your home screen. Everything else keeps running.

<Tip>
  **Want to ease in?** Under **Settings › Messaging**, switch on **Always approve the first text to a new contact**. Every first text then waits in your Inbox for you to read and send. And on anyone you would rather AgentNode left alone, switch **Managed** off: see [Managed](/contacts#managed).
</Tip>

## Questions people ask at this point

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Will it start texting my contacts right away?">
    Yes, as soon as your number can send, which is straight away on AgentNode's own approval. Texts never go out during quiet hours (9 PM to 8 AM), or on a weekend unless you allow it. To read each first text before it goes, switch on **Always approve the first text to a new contact** under **Settings › Messaging**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What do my leads see?">
    A normal text from the number you picked, written as you. The first text to each person signs off with your signature and says how to opt out, both set under **Settings › Messaging**. Nothing says AI, and nothing changes when your registration is approved.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to wait for carrier approval?">
    No. Your number texts under AgentNode's own approval while the carriers review yours, usually 1 to 3 business days. **Settings › Business** shows where it stands. If the carriers want something corrected, it says what, and AgentNode resubmits once you fix it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does it cost to run?">
    Your plan's credits pay for everything AgentNode does: texts, calls, AI and your number. One credit is one dollar. **Settings › Billing** shows what is left and where it went. When credits run out, AgentNode's own work pauses until they renew; texts from your leads still reach your Inbox. The rates are on the [pricing page](https://goagentnode.com/pricing).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I stop it texting someone?">
    Yes. Open the person under **Contacts** and switch **Managed** off. AgentNode cancels anything it had planned for them and leaves them alone until you switch it back on. Or just tell AgentNode: "stop managing everyone from Zillow".
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I got stuck. Who do I ask?">
    Press **Book onboarding call** in the menu on the left to pick a time, and a person walks you through it. Or email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com). See [Support](/support).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
