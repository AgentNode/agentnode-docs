**Operator** is the first thing you see when you open AgentNode. It is the assistant above the others: ask it anything about your contacts, conversations and bookings, or tell it what to do, and it looks it up or passes the instruction to the right assistant. The top bar carries two tabs, **Operator** and **Inbox**, with a count on the Inbox of how many things need you, and a live status on the right: "3 assistants working · updated just now".

## Asking and telling

The Operator tab opens with how many people AgentNode is looking after, then the ask box: **Ask or tell AgentNode anything about your contacts**.

- **Ask** a question and Operator searches your conversations, contacts, bookings and email to answer it: "Who asked about Fairview?", "What's booked tomorrow?", "Which Zillow leads went quiet this month?"
- **Tell** it what to do and it does it, or hands it to the assistant whose job it is: "Text everyone from Saturday's open house", "Pause Zillow leads today", "Add Marcus to past clients".

Under the box, the chips show which sources it searches, and a button shows how it acts: **Asks before it acts**. With that on, Operator checks with you before anything it cannot undo.

From **Contacts**, tick people and press **Send to Operator**: the ask box opens with "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: " already in it, and you finish the sentence.

<Tip>
  Example prompts sit under the ask box. Click one to see the kind of thing Operator can do, then write your own.
</Tip>

## The Inbox preview

Below the ask box, the Operator tab previews your Inbox: the questions and threads waiting for you, each with the assistant that asked, what it needs, and how many people are waiting on the answer. Open one to answer it, or switch to the **Inbox** tab to see everything. See [the Inbox](/inbox).

## What the assistants are doing

The live status in the top bar says how many assistants are working and when it was last updated. For the detail, open an assistant from **Your assistants** in the left nav: how its last 30 days went, and its jobs. To ask what an assistant is doing, or tell it how you like things done, ask Operator. See [Your assistants](/assistants).

<Note>
  Operator remembers what you tell it about how you like things done. Say "never text before 10" or "always mention the open house" once, and every assistant works from it. Tell it something about a lead, like "the Hendersons just had a baby", and it goes on that lead's **Relationship**, on their page, where every assistant reads it. See [Contacts](/contacts#what-agentnode-learns-about-them).
</Note>
