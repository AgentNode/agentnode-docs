**Operator** is the first thing you see when you open AgentNode. It is the assistant above the others: ask it anything about your contacts, conversations and calendar, or tell it what to do, and it looks it up or passes the instruction to the right assistant. The top bar carries three tabs, **Operator**, **Inbox** and **Jobs**, with a count on the Inbox of how many things need you, and a live status on the right: "3 assistants working · updated just now".

The same chat is on every other screen too. Press the **AgentNode** button at the bottom right, and it opens beside whatever you are looking at. Tell it what to do there, and it says what it did.

## Asking and telling

The Operator tab opens with how many contacts AgentNode is managing: **AgentNode is managing 8 contacts.** The ask box is under it: **Ask or tell AgentNode anything**. If AgentNode isn't managing anyone yet, it opens on that instead, with the way to start first. See [Who AgentNode is managing](#who-agentnode-is-managing).

- **Ask** a question and Operator searches your conversations, contacts, calendar and email to answer it: "Who asked about Fairview?", "What's booked tomorrow?", "Which Zillow leads went quiet this month?"
- **Tell** it what to do and it does it, or hands it to the assistant whose job it is: "Text everyone from Saturday's open house", "Stop managing my Zillow leads", "Add Marcus to past clients". Before it switches **Managed** for a whole group, it tells you how many people that is and waits for your yes. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).
- **Pass on news** about a lead and the assistants whose jobs it concerns act on it: "Pat is ready to look again in the spring", "Jo didn't show up to the viewing".
- **Work in your CRM**: with Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel connected, Operator searches it and reads a person's whole record there: "Who in Follow Up Boss is tagged Relocation?" Ask and it adds a note, puts a tag on or takes one off, or adds a task there. Before it puts someone on a Follow Up Boss action plan or a GoHighLevel workflow, it tells you which one and waits for your yes, and it never does for anyone who opted out or is marked do not contact. Each part has its own switch on the CRM under **Settings › Integrations**: **Operator search** for looking things up, **Operator updates** for changes. See [What your CRM is used for](/settings#what-your-crm-is-used-for).
- **Show** it photos and screenshots, up to five with a message: paste them into the ask box, drop them on it, or pick them with the photo button. Operator reads them along with what you write: "add her" with a contact card, "draft him a reply" with a lead's email, "book this" with a showing request. Send a contact card or a lead notification on its own and Operator adds the person on it. See [Adding a single contact](/contacts#adding-a-single-contact).

Before it does anything it cannot take back, such as switching **Managed** for a whole group or starting one of your CRM's automations, Operator tells you what it is about to do and waits for your yes.

From **Contacts**, tick people and press **Send to Operator**: the ask box opens with "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: " already in it, and you finish the sentence.

<Tip>
  Example prompts sit under the ask box. Click one to see the kind of thing Operator can do, then write your own.
</Tip>

## The Inbox preview

Below the ask box, the Operator tab previews your Inbox: the newest two things waiting for you, each with the assistant that asked, what it needs, and how long it has waited. Open one to answer it, or switch to the **Inbox** tab to see everything. See [the Inbox](/inbox).

## Who AgentNode is managing

Until you start a conversation, the Operator tab also shows who AgentNode is managing, under **AgentNode is managing**. You see the faces of the 10 managed contacts AgentNode texted or added most recently, newest first. Under each face is what AgentNode last did, such as **Texted 4m** or **Added 2d**. While an assistant is working on someone, it says what it is doing instead, such as **Planning**. Press a face to open that contact's page. With more than 10, press **See all** to open **Contacts** with only the managed ones. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).

If AgentNode isn't managing anyone yet, the tab opens with **AgentNode isn't managing anyone yet.** and the way to start comes first, above the ask box. AgentNode does nothing for your contacts until some are managed, so this is the one thing to do. Press **Choose who to manage** to see your contacts, newest first. Search for someone by name, phone or email. Or narrow the list with the buttons under the search box, such as **Recently added**, or pick one of your lists, tags or sources. Then switch **Managed** on for each contact you want AgentNode to manage. The assistants start on them at once, so they may get a text soon. Each switch saves as you press it, and you can switch it back off the same way. Press **Done** when you're finished. Or press **Add contact** to add someone new. To manage a whole group at once, tell Operator who, like "manage my Zillow leads". With no contacts yet, it shows the ways to add them: **Import CSV**, **Connect your CRM** and **Add contact**.

## Jobs

A job is something you want AgentNode to do later, once or again and again. You say what, and when. AgentNode works out how each time it runs.

- **Once**: "Tomorrow at 10, text Dana that the open house moved to 3".
- **Again and again**: "Every Monday at 9, send me who went quiet last week".
- **Around meetings**: "A week before every listing appointment, send the seller a CMA figure". This runs before each meeting with a lead.

To add one, type it in the **New job** box on the **Jobs** tab and press **Add**, or just tell Operator. AgentNode answers under the box with what it took you to mean.

When a job runs, AgentNode uses your connected tools to do it, then tells you what it did in the Operator chat and on your phone. It may also email you, or ask you a question in your [Inbox](/inbox). Answer the question and the job runs again with your answer. Texts to leads still follow consent and opt-outs.

The **Jobs** tab lists your jobs under **Coming up**, **Around meetings** and **Done**. Each shows when it runs, when it runs next, and what it did last time.

- Switch a job off to pause it and keep it for later.
- To remove one for good, press **⋯**, then **Delete job**.

## What the assistants are doing

The live status in the top bar says how many assistants are working and when it was last updated. For the detail, open an assistant from **Your assistants** in the menu on the left: how its last 30 days went, and its jobs. To ask what an assistant is doing, or tell it how you like things done, ask Operator. See [Your assistants](/assistants).

<Note>
  Operator remembers what you tell it about how you like things done. Say "never text before 10" or "always mention the open house" once, and every assistant works from it. Tell it something about a lead, like "the Hendersons just had a baby", and it is kept for that lead, where every assistant reads it. See [How AgentNode learns you](/learning).
</Note>
