**Operator** is the first thing you see when you open AgentNode. It is the assistant above the others: ask it anything about your contacts, conversations and calendar, or tell it what to do, and it looks it up or passes the instruction to the right assistant. Your [Inbox](/inbox) and your [Jobs](#jobs) are beside it.

The same chat is on every other screen too. Press the **AgentNode** button at the bottom right, and it opens beside whatever you are looking at. Tell it what to do there, and it says what it did.

## Asking and telling

Operator says how many contacts AgentNode is managing, and asks you to tell it anything. If AgentNode isn't managing anyone yet, it asks you to start there first. See [Who AgentNode is managing](#who-agentnode-is-managing).

- **Ask** a question and Operator searches your conversations, contacts, calendar and email to answer it: "Who asked about Fairview?", "What's booked tomorrow?", "Which Zillow leads went quiet this month?"
- **Ask about your numbers**, like "How many leads replied each week this month?", and Operator draws a chart under its answer when one makes it clearer. Every figure on it comes from your own contacts and conversations. Press **Figures** to see them as a table.
- **Tell** it what to do and it does it, or hands it to the assistant whose job it is: "Text everyone from Saturday's open house", "Stop managing my Zillow leads", "Add Marcus to past clients". Before it switches **Managed** for a whole group, it tells you how many people that is and waits for your yes. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).
- **Text a lead** and Operator writes it to fit that person's conversation, so it reads as part of it, and its words can differ from yours: if they've already heard from you, it won't introduce you again, and if they wrote last, it picks up what they said first. The chat shows the words that were sent.
- **Text a lead in quiet hours** (9 PM to 8 AM, your time or the lead's, going by their area code) and Operator shows you the words and asks first: send now, or when quiet hours end. A text it holds waits in the lead's conversation and goes between 8 and 8:30 AM; Operator tells you the time. If the lead writes before you answer, Operator writes the text again and asks again.
- **Text a group**, like "Text everyone in San Diego about my open house on Saturday", and Operator tells you how many people that is and about what it costs, then waits for your yes. It texts them a few at a time as a job on the **Jobs** tab, writing each text to fit that person's conversation. Anyone whose conversation had a text in the last day, or is waiting on you, is skipped so they are not texted twice. The job names them. Tell it to stop, or switch the job off, and nobody else is texted.
- **Pass on news** about a lead and the assistants whose jobs it concerns act on it: "Pat is ready to look again in the spring", "Jo didn't show up to the viewing".
- **Work in your CRM**: with Follow Up Boss or GoHighLevel connected, Operator searches it and reads a person's whole record there: "Who in Follow Up Boss is tagged Relocation?" Ask and it adds a note, puts a tag on or takes one off, or adds a task there. Before it puts someone on a Follow Up Boss action plan or a GoHighLevel workflow, it tells you which one and waits for your yes, and it never does for anyone who opted out or is marked do not contact. Each part has its own switch on the CRM under **Settings › Integrations**: **Operator search** for looking things up, **Operator updates** for changes. See [What your CRM is used for](/settings#what-your-crm-is-used-for).
- **Show** it photos, screenshots and files, up to twenty with a message, five of them photos. Operator reads them along with what you write: "add her" with a contact card, "draft him a reply" with a lead's email, "book this" with a showing request. Send a contact card or a lead notification on its own and Operator adds the person on it. See [Adding a single contact](/contacts#adding-a-single-contact).
- **Send a CSV of new leads** with "import these new leads". Operator says how many people it found and asks what an import needs: that they agreed to be texted, whether they are new leads, and whether AgentNode manages them. On your yes it imports them as **Import from CSV** does. A PDF or text file is read too. A file can hold some thousands of rows; for a bigger one, use **Import from CSV**. See [Importing a CSV](/contacts#importing-a-csv).
- **Speak** instead of typing. Press the microphone in the box and say what you want. Your words appear as you talk, so you can read and fix them before you send. Your browser does the listening, so it costs nothing. It works in most browsers, but not Firefox.

Before it does anything it cannot take back, such as switching **Managed** for a whole group or starting one of your CRM's automations, Operator tells you what it is about to do and waits for your yes.

From **Contacts**, tick people and press **Send to Operator** to tell it what to do with them.

## The Inbox preview

Operator also shows the newest things waiting for you in the Inbox, each with the assistant that asked, what it needs, and how long it has waited, so you can answer without leaving it. See [the Inbox](/inbox).

## Who AgentNode is managing

Until you start a conversation, Operator also shows who AgentNode is managing: the managed contacts it texted or added most recently, and what it last did for each. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).

If AgentNode isn't managing anyone yet, Operator asks you to start there, because it does nothing for your contacts until some are managed. **Choose who to manage** opens your contacts, to search or narrow them and switch **Managed** on for each person you want, one by one. As each goes on, AgentNode asks if there is anything it should know first, which you can skip. The assistants start on them at once, so they may get a text soon. To manage a whole group at once, tell Operator who, like "manage my Zillow leads". With no contacts yet, it offers the ways to add them: a CSV file, your CRM, or one by hand. Either way, under the buttons you can **Book an onboarding call**, if you would rather we walk you through it, or **ask a question**.

## Jobs

A job is something you want AgentNode to do later, once or again and again. You say what, and when. AgentNode works out how each time it runs.

- **Once**: "Tomorrow at 10, text Dana that the open house moved to 3".
- **Again and again**: "Every Monday at 9, send me who went quiet last week".
- **Around meetings**: "A week before every listing appointment, send the seller a CMA figure". This runs before each listing appointment, and leaves your other meetings alone.

To add one, type it on the **Jobs** tab, or just tell Operator. AgentNode answers with what it took you to mean.

When a job runs, AgentNode uses your connected tools to do it, then tells you what it did in the Operator chat and on your phone. It may also email you, or ask you a question in your [Inbox](/inbox). Answer the question and the job runs again with your answer. Texts to leads still follow consent and opt-outs.

The **Jobs** tab lists your jobs by name: coming up, around meetings and done. Pick one to see when it runs, what it does and what it did last time. AgentNode names each job; to rename one, tell Operator. Switch a job off to pause it and keep it for later, or delete it for good.

## What the assistants are doing

AgentNode says how many assistants are working at the moment. For the detail, open an assistant under **Your assistants** in the menu on the left: how its last 30 days went, and its jobs. To ask what an assistant is doing, or tell it how you like things done, ask Operator. See [Your assistants](/assistants).

<Note>
  Operator remembers what you tell it about how you like things done. Say "never text before 10" or "always mention the open house" once, and every assistant works from it. Tell it something about a lead, like "the Hendersons just had a baby", and it is kept for that lead, where every assistant reads it. See [How AgentNode learns you](/learning).
</Note>
