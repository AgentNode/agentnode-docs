# Getting started

AgentNode follows up with your leads by text, in your voice, and hands the conversation to you the moment it needs you.

## Sign up

Create your account, then tell AgentNode two things: what you message leads about, and when you send. That is what it works from. You can change either later in **Settings**.

## Import your contacts

Export your contacts from wherever you keep them today — your CRM or a spreadsheet — as a CSV, then use **Import CSV** from the Inbox. AgentNode reads names, numbers and any notes. Importing ends by asking what you want to do with the people you just brought in.

## Where things are

- **Inbox** — what needs you, your conversations, and your bookings.
- **Contacts** — everyone you have imported, with their tags and their conversation beside them.
- **Campaigns** — the standing work AgentNode does for a set of contacts.
- **Settings** — your account, your number and what AgentNode sends.
