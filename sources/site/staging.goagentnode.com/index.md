How it works

## Three steps from import to appointment.

1. 1

 Import your contacts from a CSVExport your existing database from any source — your CRM, spreadsheet, or wherever you keep contacts — and upload it. AgentNode reads the names, numbers, and any notes you have.
2. 2

 AI drafts a personalised message for each leadAgentNode writes an individual follow-up text for every contact, referencing what you know about them and the current market conditions relevant to their area.
3. 3

 Review, send, and book the repliesYou read every draft before it goes anywhere. Approve or edit, then send. When leads respond, you book the appointment — AgentNode handles the campaign, you handle the relationship.

Features

## Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Database reactivationEvery dormant contact in your database gets surfaced and worked — not just the ones you happened to remember last week.

AI-drafted messagingEach message is written to that specific person, drawing on what you know about them and conditions in their neighbourhood.

Guardrails that escalateAgentNode answers what it can and hands the rest to you. Price, contracts and anything off-script stop AgentNode and land in your queue.

On-demand CMA generatorWhen a conversation heats up, generate a comparable market analysis for that lead's area in one click — ready to share before the call.

Guardrails

## It texts on its own, and knows when to stop.

AgentNode runs the campaign without you in the loop. The guardrails decide what it will not answer — price, contracts, anything it reads as unclear — and those conversations come straight to you.

- Sends and replies on its ownThe opener goes out on your cadence and AgentNode answers the easy questions — timing, neighbourhoods, what you have coming up — without waiting on you.
- Hands off when it cannot answerCommission, offer terms, anything legal, or a reply it reads as unclear: AgentNode stops, flags the reason, and puts the contact at the top of your queue.
- Consent recorded at importWhen you upload your CSV, you confirm that your contacts are people you have a legitimate prior relationship with. That record stays on file.
- Opt-outs are tracked immediatelyAny contact who replies to stop is removed from your active queue right away. Their status is logged so they won't surface again in future runs.

One switch on every conversation. Flip it and you're driving; flip it back and AgentNode picks up where you left off.
