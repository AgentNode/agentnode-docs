# Contacts

A contact is a lead you have imported and want AgentNode to manage.

## Importing

**Import CSV** takes a file of any size. Map its columns once, and AgentNode remembers the mapping for next time. When the import finishes it asks what to do next: reach out, add them to a campaign, or nothing yet.

## Adding one

**Add contact** is two steps: who they are, then what AgentNode should do with them — send a first text now, add them to campaigns, both, or neither.

## Tags

A tag is a label like _from zillow_ or _inner circle_. A contact can carry several, one for each context they are in. Pick a tag at the top of the contacts list to see just those people.

## Reaching out

Select one or more contacts and press **Reach out**. A new campaign opens with them already added, so all that is left to say is what it should do.

## Opting out

A lead who replies **STOP** is opted out straight away. AgentNode never texts them again, and no campaign can pick them up.
