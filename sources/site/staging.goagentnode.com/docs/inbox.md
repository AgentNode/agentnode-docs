# Inbox

The Inbox is where your day starts. It has three columns.

## Needs you

Campaigns asking for something, and conversations AgentNode stopped on because a reply needed a person. Open one to see what was asked and answer it.

## Conversations

Every thread, most recent first, with the last thing said. Open one to read it and reply yourself. AgentNode keeps handling the thread unless you tell it to stop.

## Bookings

Appointments leads have booked: the most recent, then the week ahead by day.

## Quick actions

Above the columns: **Add contact**, **Import CSV**, **Reach out** and **New campaign**. Reach out sends one text to one person, from your number, as you — write it or let AgentNode draft it from their history.
