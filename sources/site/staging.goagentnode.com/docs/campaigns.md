# Campaigns

A campaign is an automated job that runs for a set of contacts. You say what you want AgentNode to do and who it runs for, and the campaign keeps doing it — for the contacts it names, and for anyone added later — until you stop it.

For example: _Past clients: check in with each of them every three months._

## Creating one

**New campaign** asks two questions: what you want AgentNode to do, then who it should run for. The campaign opens on its chat and asks there for anything else it needs, like birthdays.

## The workspace

Each campaign has four tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What you asked for and how it is going: who is on it, what it sent, who replied, who booked. |
| Recent messages | The texts it has sent and the replies. |
| Contacts | Who is on it, and who it took off and why. |
| Context | Its files, and the live sources it can look things up in. |

The campaign's chat is always beside them. Tell it to change course there.

## Pausing and stopping

Pause a campaign and nothing more is sent until you resume it. Contacts stay on it. A campaign never messages someone who has opted out.
