Settings is where you tell AgentNode who you are and how you work. The assistants draw on your profile for every text they write, and on the messaging rules for every text they send, so keeping these accurate is the most direct way to make your outreach sound like you.

## Account

Your name, work email address and sign-in method. AgentNode supports two ways to sign in:

- **Email code**: enter your email address and AgentNode sends a one-time code. No password to remember.
- **Password**: an email and password, if you would rather use one.

**Appearance** sets how AgentNode looks. **System**, the default, follows your device's light or dark mode and switches when it does. **Light** and **Dark** keep it one way. Each browser keeps its own choice, so two computers you sign in on can differ.

## Realtor profile

The assistants' brief on you: your brokerage, your market, how you like to text, and anything you always or never bring up. Every text draws on it.

## Business

Your business details, used wherever a text or a signature needs them.

## Billing

AgentNode runs on credits, which pay for texts, calls and the AI behind them. Top up here and see what has been used. The work the assistants do for your managed contacts draws on them as it happens: see [What managing costs](/contacts#what-managing-costs).

**Daily AI limit** caps how many credits the assistants can spend on AI each day. When they reach it, they pause until midnight. Raise it to let them carry on now. Anything you ask Operator to do still works.

When your credits run out, the assistants pause until they renew. A lead who texts meanwhile comes to your [Inbox](/inbox), where **Answer now** answers them anyway.

## Operator

How Operator acts on what you ask: whether it checks with you before anything it cannot undo.

## Messaging

### Sending

- **Sending number**: the number every text goes from, and whether it is ready to text. If it isn't, it says why. To pick or buy a number, go to **Settings › Numbers**.
- **Signature**: the name the first text to each person opens with and signs off with; after that, it never signs again.

### Rules

Every text follows these, whichever assistant wrote it:

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Always approve the first text to a new contact** | The opener waits in your Inbox until you approve it. Follow-ups send as usual. |
| **Quiet hours** | Nothing AgentNode writes sends between 9 PM and 8 AM. You choose what happens to a text due then: it waits in your Inbox for you to check in the morning (the default), it sends at 8 AM, or it is not sent. |
| **Opt-out line on first text** | The first text to each person carries "Reply STOP to opt out". |
| **Send on weekends** | On, a text due on a Saturday or Sunday goes then. Off, it waits for Monday morning. Replies to leads who text you still go. |

## Integrations

The apps AgentNode can connect to: your calendar (Google, Outlook or Apple), Follow Up Boss, and, coming soon, GoHighLevel, MLS (ACTRIS), ShowingTime, Zoom and Gmail. Your assistants can use any app connected here, and **Add integration** on a job lands on this page. See [Your assistants](/assistants#add-ons).

To connect Follow Up Boss, open it and press **Sign in**. **Other ways to connect** has a way to paste a key instead, for when signing in doesn't work for you. See [Contacts](/contacts#switching-an-import-on).

### Your calendar

Connect one calendar, and AgentNode adds the meetings it books to it and only offers times you are free. AgentNode only sees when you are busy, never what your events are.

| Calendar | How you connect it |
|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | Sign in to Google. |
| **Outlook Calendar** | Sign in to Microsoft: a work or school account for Microsoft 365, or a personal one for Outlook.com. Video calls get a Teams link where your Microsoft 365 plan has Teams. |
| **Apple Calendar** | Your Apple ID and an app-specific password, a separate password Apple makes for one app. Make the password at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/account/manage) under **Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords**, name it AgentNode, and paste it in. Your Apple ID password does not work here. |

AgentNode keeps one calendar at a time. To switch, disconnect the one you have, then connect the other. Choose which of your calendars meetings go into under **Settings › Calendar**.

## When changes take effect

Changes take effect on the **next text** AgentNode sends. Texts already sent stay as they are, so you can refine your profile and rules at any time.
