Settings is where you tell AgentNode who you are and how you work. Open it from the menu on the left.

## Account

Who is signed in, and the basics.

- **Your name**: what AgentNode calls you when it first texts a new lead. Press **Change name** to fix it, or **Add your name** if there is none. Your email is the one you sign in with, and can't be changed.
- **Your phone number**: under your email. Press **Add phone number**, enter your mobile number and press **Text me a code**, then enter the code AgentNode texts you. Then you can sign in with a code AgentNode texts you, and [text AgentNode](/texting-agentnode). **Change** and **Remove** sit beside it. US numbers only.
- **Confirm your email**: shown only if you signed up with a texted code. AgentNode emails you a code to confirm it, and then you can sign in by email too.
- **Password**: AgentNode signs you in with an emailed code, so you never need one. You can set one anyway, of at least 12 characters, once a code confirms it is you.
- **Time zone**: picked up from your browser. Quiet hours and booking hours run in it, so check it is right.
- **Appearance**: **System**, the default, follows your device's light or dark mode. **Light** and **Dark** keep it one way. Each browser keeps its own choice.
- **Delete account**: wipes your contacts, conversations and settings, releases your texting numbers, deletes your carrier registration and ends your subscription. It cannot be undone. Before it goes, AgentNode asks why you're leaving. You can tick a reason or say more, or skip it.

## Realtor profile

Facts about you and your business: your brokerage, your areas, your languages, how long you have been licensed. If AgentNode read your website during setup, some are already here. Your assistants quote a fact when a lead asks and never volunteer one, so add anything a lead might ask about.

## Billing

- **Plan**: your subscription, and when it renews. **Manage** opens Stripe's own page to change your card or see invoices.
- **Cancel plan**: your plan stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and you aren't charged again. After that AgentNode stops working your leads, and 30 days later your number is released. AgentNode asks why first, and you can skip it. Changed your mind? **Keep my plan** undoes it any time before the plan ends.
- **Credits**: what is left this period, and what AI, texts, calls and property lookups have cost. One credit is one dollar. Your plan's credits renew with it; credits you buy carry over. The work the assistants do for your managed contacts draws on them as it happens: see [What managing costs](/contacts#what-managing-costs).
- **Buy credits**: add any amount from 10 to 1,000 credits now, charged to the card on your plan. You confirm the amount before you're charged.
- **Auto recharge**: when your credits fall below the amount you set, AgentNode buys the number of credits you set on the card on your plan, so your work doesn't pause. It tops up at most five times a day, ten minutes apart. If your card is declined it stops and says so; update your card and save to try again. Every purchase, by hand or by auto recharge, is confirmed by email.
- **Daily AI limit**: the most AgentNode's own work spends on AI in a day. Reaching it pauses that work until midnight; raising it lets what is waiting go now. What you ask for yourself still works.

When you're under 1 credit, everything that costs credits stops, the assistants included, until you buy more or they renew. A lead who texts meanwhile comes to your [Inbox](/inbox), where **Answer now** answers them anyway.

## Business

The business your texts are registered to, and where that registration stands with the carriers, in a word: **In review**, **Approved**, **Resubmitted**, **Refused** or **Appealed**, and since when. If the carriers send your details back, this page says what to correct, and AgentNode resubmits once you have. Approval usually takes 1 to 3 business days.

If you uploaded the IRS letter for a new EIN, AgentNode sends it to the carriers whenever they cannot match your EIN to your business name. If they turn that appeal down, texting under AgentNode's own approval stops straight away, and you get an email saying what to check. To text again, press **Correct your details** and fix anything that does not match the letter: texting starts again while the carriers look. Or upload a better copy of the letter there, and texting starts again once the carriers approve you. If you already text under your own carrier approval, you keep texting.

## Numbers

The numbers your texts can go from. Your leads text back the one they heard from.

- **Your numbers**: each number, whether it is active, and **Release** for one you no longer need. The number you send from, and the one your registration was filed with, stay, and your account always keeps at least one.
- **Buy a number**: enter an area code and pick from the numbers suggested. It costs credits, and then a few credits a month.

## Messaging

### Sending

- **Sending number**: the number every text goes from, and what it is doing: texting since when, or why it is not yet. Buy or change it under Numbers.

### Rules

Every text follows these, whichever assistant wrote it:

| Rule | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Manually approve the first plan for each lead** | Each lead's first plan waits in your Inbox, and nothing reaches them until you approve it. Once you do, its texts and bookings go without asking you again, and plans after it go ahead on their own. See [Approving first plans](/contacts#approving-first-plans). |
| **Quiet hours** | Nothing sends on its own in these hours. You choose what becomes of a text due then: it waits with your drafts, goes at 8 AM, or is not sent. If you choose drafts, a reply to a lead who texted in the evening or overnight waits only for 8 AM, then goes as your **Replies** settings allow. |
| **Opt-out line on first text** | The first text to each person ends with how to opt out, such as "Reply STOP to opt out". You can change the wording. |
| **Send on weekends** | On, a text due on a Saturday or Sunday goes then. Off, it waits for Monday morning. An answer to a lead still goes. |

A text you send yourself always goes, whatever the rules say. Nobody is texted without consent on file.

### Replies

- **Reply for me**: whether AgentNode answers a lead's texts on its own. On until you turn it off.
- **Confidence**: how much you trust AgentNode to reply without you: **Low**, **Medium**, **High** (the default) or **Every time**. The higher it is, the more replies send on their own. The rest wait in your Inbox as drafts. Even at Every time, a reply AgentNode cannot vouch for waits for you.
- **Playfulness**: the tone, from plain and brief to chatty. Nothing else changes: texts stay short, with no emoji.

## Calendar

- **Your calendar**: the calendar you connected, and **Book into**, which of its calendars new meetings go in. Only your busy times are read, never what your events are.
- **Booking rules**: **Booking hours**, the hours AgentNode may offer and book meetings in, and how long **a call** and **a viewing** last in your diary.

## Integrations

Google Calendar, Outlook Calendar, Apple Calendar, Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, KW Command, BoldTrail, Prime Listing Leads, MLS (ACTRIS), ShowingTime, Zoom and Gmail, plus Claude and ChatGPT. Press one to connect it. A job's add-ons and working preferences pick their tool from the ones connected here. See [Your assistants](/assistants#add-ons).

### What your CRM is used for

Open a connected CRM, such as Follow Up Boss, to see what AgentNode uses it for. Each use has its own switch under **What it's used for**:

- **Import contacts**: new people come into Contacts, with their birthdays and home anniversaries. The first time, you answer a few questions and confirm they agreed to hear from you by text. It is the same switch as the CRM's row on the **Auto-import** tab.
- **Operator search**: Operator looks people up in your CRM when you ask, and the assistants read a lead's record there.
- **Operator updates**: Operator adds notes, tags and tasks in your CRM, only when you ask. In Follow Up Boss it also moves people's stages and starts action plans.

**Configure**, beside the **Import contacts** switch, asks the same questions as **Configure** on the **Auto-import** tab: whether to import the contacts already in your CRM, the tags they get, and whether AgentNode manages them. See [Changing an import](/contacts#changing-an-import).

KW Command, BoldTrail and Prime Listing Leads have **Import contacts** only. Prime Listing Leads needs no key or sign-in: AgentNode finds your account there by your email or mobile number and connects it for you, with its import on. **Connect Prime Listing Leads** looks now, if it has not found you yet. See [Prime Listing Leads](/contacts#prime-listing-leads).

### Your calendar

Connect one calendar, and AgentNode adds the meetings it books to it and only offers times you are free.

| Calendar | How you connect it |
|---|---|
| **Google Calendar** | Sign in to Google. |
| **Outlook Calendar** | Sign in to Microsoft: a work or school account for Microsoft 365, or a personal one for Outlook.com. Video calls get a Teams link where your Microsoft 365 plan has Teams. |
| **Apple Calendar** | Your Apple ID and an app-specific password. Make the password at [account.apple.com](https://account.apple.com/account/manage) under **Sign-In and Security › App-Specific Passwords**, name it AgentNode, and paste it in. Your usual Apple ID password does not work here. |

AgentNode keeps one calendar at a time. To switch, disconnect the one you have, then connect the other.

### Claude and ChatGPT

Connect Claude or ChatGPT to ask it about your contacts, conversations and calendar, or to have it act on them for you. You sign in to AgentNode once, and never give it your AgentNode password. Everyone on a team signs in as themselves, so each person sees only their own contacts.

Open **Claude** or **ChatGPT** here, copy the **Connector URL**, and follow its **Set-up steps**:

- **Claude**: in Claude, open **Settings › Connectors**, choose **Add custom connector** and paste the URL. It works in Claude on the web, desktop and mobile.
- **ChatGPT**: in ChatGPT on the web, open **Plugins**, press **+** and choose **Add custom MCP server**. Name it AgentNode, paste the URL, choose **OAuth**, tick **I understand and want to continue** and press **Create as a plugin**. Then open the plugin and press **+** to install it. To use it, switch ChatGPT's home page from **Chat** to **Work** and type **@AgentNode** in a new chat. On a ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu plan, an admin may need to allow this first. If your ChatGPT looks different, follow [OpenAI's guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12584461-developer-mode-and-mcp-apps-in-chatgpt).

When it asks you to sign in to AgentNode, press **Allow**. To disconnect, press **Disconnect** beside it here, or remove it in Claude or ChatGPT. A connection that goes unused for 90 days ends by itself.

If this is too much, email support@goagentnode.com or press **Book onboarding call** in the app.

## Channels

Where AgentNode reaches you. **SMS channel** lets you text AgentNode from your phone and run your account that way: confirm your mobile number with a code AgentNode texts you, if it doesn't have it yet, then press **Turn on**, and AgentNode texts you hello. See [Text AgentNode from your phone](/texting-agentnode).

**iPhone app** sets which notifications reach your phone: questions from your Inbox, texts from leads, and calendar changes. See [The iPhone app](/iphone-app).

## Refer a friend

Your referral link and code: share either, and when a friend signs up with it and pays, your next month is on us, $97 off your bill, for every friend who joins. **Invite by email** sends them your link from AgentNode, with your name on it, and **People you referred** lists everyone who used it and whether they have paid yet.

## Changing settings

Changes take effect on the next text AgentNode sends. Conversations already under way are not rewritten.
