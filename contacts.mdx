Contacts is where everyone you have brought into AgentNode lives, with their tags, whether AgentNode manages them, and their conversation right beside them. The top bar has two tabs. **Contacts** lists everyone. **Auto-import** shows the apps, like your CRM, that bring new people in by themselves. **Add contacts** sits at the right, with **Add manually** and **Import from CSV**.

Press **Name** or **Last contacted** at the head of the list to sort by it; press again to reverse it, and a third time for the usual order. **Filters** narrows the list to **Managed** or **Not managed** people, among others.

## Managed

Every contact is **Managed** or not, and the list says which. A managed contact is one AgentNode's assistants text and answer for you; for anyone else, AgentNode does nothing on its own. Everyone you add or import starts out managed. Switch it off with the **Managed** switch at the top of **About** on their page, and back on whenever you like. Nobody who has opted out is texted, managed or not.

In the iPhone app, the **Managed** switch is on each contact's page, and the filter button at the top of **Contacts** shows **Everyone**, **Managed** or **Not managed**.

To switch many at once, tell [Operator](/operator): "stop managing everyone from Zillow", "turn off managing all contacts", "manage my past clients again". For everyone, a tag or a source, Operator first tells you how many people it will switch, and switches them only when you say yes. Switching someone off cancels the texts the assistants had planned for them. Switching people back on, however many, has the assistants start on each of them straight away, so they may get a text soon. Whether a text sends by itself or waits for your OK depends on **Settings › Messaging**.

### What managing costs

Your credits go on the work AgentNode does for your managed contacts. For each one, the assistants answer every text they send, check on them while they are quiet (every few days for a new lead, once a month for one gone cold), and write and send their texts. Each of these uses credits, at the rates on the [pricing page](https://goagentnode.com/pricing). People cost the most in their first days: the assistants start on them straight away, and most get a first text.

A contact who is not managed costs nothing while they are quiet: no assistant looks at them or texts them. A text they send you still uses a little, to receive it and learn from it, and so does a text you send them yourself.

So keep Managed on for the people you want AgentNode to work, and off for everyone else, such as an old list you keep for reference. Credits spent on people you don't need followed up are credits a new lead may need. When your credits run out, AgentNode stops working, replies included, until they renew. See [Billing](/settings#billing).

## A contact's page

Open any contact and their page has three columns:

- **About**: the **Managed** switch, their details, **Your note** and, under **Relationship**, what AgentNode has learned about them. The assistants read your note, but never put it in a text.
- **Conversation**: their latest texts, each captioned **Automatic** when an assistant sent it, **AI draft** when you sent one AgentNode wrote, or **You**. Press the AI logo on one of AgentNode's texts to say what to correct, or why it was wrong.
- **Notes** and **Files**. Only you see notes: AgentNode never reads them, so they cannot end up in a text.

Under the conversation, the **Next** card shows the next text an assistant has planned for them and when, or why none is coming. Its arrow opens **Upcoming messages**: what has happened with them so far, then every text coming. **Send now instead** opens the reply box on that next text. Send it as it is or in your own words, and it goes right away instead of the planned one.

Below it, press **Write a text** to open the reply box.

## What AgentNode learns about them

AgentNode learns about each contact in the background, as things happen. When a lead texts something about themselves that will still matter in a month, such as a move in March, a new budget or a birthday, it goes on their page under **Relationship**, with where it came from: "From Dana's reply, Thu, Oct 8, 9:31 AM". Tell Operator something about a lead, like "Dana's moving to Leeds in March", and it goes there too, as does what an assistant learns while it works for them: "Noted by Lead Nurture · Follow-up, Thu, Oct 8, 9:31 AM". Every assistant reads a contact's Relationship each time it works for them, so what one learns, they all remember.

AgentNode learns this for every contact, whether or not their Managed switch is on. Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are kept private: the assistants take them into account, but never mention them in a text. Anything you typed yourself stays as you wrote it, and anything can come off with its **×**.

## Importing a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your contacts">
    Download your contacts as a CSV file from your CRM, spreadsheet or lead source. CSV is a simple spreadsheet file that most CRMs and Excel can save. AgentNode accepts files of any size, so you do not need to split large lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it from Contacts">
    Press **Add contacts** in the top bar of Contacts, then **Import from CSV**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Match the columns">
    Tell AgentNode which column is the name, which is the phone number, and which columns hold notes it should know about. Next time you upload a file laid out the same way, it remembers your choices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what AgentNode should do with them">
    Turn on **They are new leads** (off to start with) if these people just got in touch with you. Lead Nurture's first text then answers them, and it follows up until they reply or book. **Managed by AgentNode** (on) has the assistants start on everyone straight away, new leads or not, so most get a first text; switch it off to save them as contacts only, which costs nothing while they are quiet. See [What managing costs](#what-managing-costs).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. AgentNode saves your confirmation, and texts nobody without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AgentNode skips duplicates. If a phone number is already in your contacts, that person is not added twice.
</Note>

## Adding a single contact

Press **Add contacts**, then **Add manually**. Give their name and number, and their source if you know it, and tick that they gave you permission to text them. Then say who they are:

- **A new lead**: Lead Nurture sends the first text and follows up until they reply or book.
- **An old lead or past contact**: Re-engagement checks in with one low-pressure question, with no first-contact intro.

**Managed by AgentNode** is on unless you switch it off; off, they are saved as a contact only and nothing is sent until you turn it on. **Just save the contact** saves them that way straight away.

Or tell [Operator](/operator) who they are: "Add Sam Lee, 555 010 0100, met at Saturday's open house, happy to get texts." Say that they agreed to hear from you by text. If you don't, they are saved, but not texted until they agree. Say too whether they are a new inquiry, someone who has just reached out to you: only then does Lead Nurture's **New lead** text them, and Operator asks when it cannot tell. They are saved as managed. Nothing is sent the moment they are added: the assistants reach out on their usual schedule.

Or show Operator a screenshot: a contact card, a lead notification, a business card or an email signature. Paste it into the ask box, drop it there or pick it with the photo button; on the iPhone, send it with **+**. Operator adds each person it shows, exactly as written, then tells you what it saved and asks for anything it could not read clearly. A photo doesn't count as permission to text, so say so if they agreed.

## Acting on several people

Tick people in the list and a bar appears above it: **Send to Operator**, **Add to list**, **Add to tag** and **Delete**. Send to Operator opens Operator with their names already in the ask box, "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: ", so you finish the sentence with what to do. See [Operator](/operator).

## Auto-import

Auto-import brings people in from your CRM by itself, so you don't have to keep uploading files. Once a day AgentNode checks your CRM for everyone you chose, and where your CRM allows it, new people come in the moment they are added there.

The **Auto-import** tab lists the sources you have connected: Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel and a Slack channel. Each row shows how many people it brought in this week and when it last checked, then its **source** and the **tag** it gives everyone it brings in. CSV imports and people added through Operator are not listed here.

Under **Add an import** are the ones you have not connected yet, each taking you to **Settings › Integrations**.

### Switching an import on

You connect Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel or Slack once, under **Settings › Integrations**. For a CRM, press **Sign in**, sign in to your CRM and press **Allow**. GoHighLevel asks which of your accounts to connect (it calls them sub-accounts): pick the one your leads are in.

Connecting brings nobody in yet. To start, turn on the CRM's switch on the **Auto-import** tab, and choose who comes in. The first time, confirm that the people in it agreed to hear from you by text. Anyone your CRM marks as do not contact is skipped.

While an import is on, its row shows what the last check found, and **Import now** checks again. Switching it off stops the checks and keeps everyone already brought in.

<Accordion title="Can't sign in? Connect with a key instead">
  Under **Settings › Integrations**, open your CRM and press **Other ways to connect**.

  - **Follow Up Boss**: in Follow Up Boss, open **Admin › API**, press **Create API Key**, and paste it into AgentNode. If the account owner makes the key, new people come in the moment they are added.
  - **GoHighLevel**: in GoHighLevel, open **Settings › Private Integrations** and create one with **View Contacts** ticked (add **Edit Contacts** and **View Workflows** for AgentNode to add notes, tags and tasks when you ask). Paste it into AgentNode with your **Location ID**, from **Settings › Business Profile**.
</Accordion>

### New people the moment they are added

- **Follow Up Boss**: new people come in the moment they are added when the account owner connected it. If someone else connected it, they come in with the once-a-day check.
- **GoHighLevel**: this needs a one-time setup inside GoHighLevel. Ask whoever manages your GoHighLevel, or follow the steps below.

<Accordion title="Optional: set up GoHighLevel to send new people straight away">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Create the workflow">
      In GoHighLevel, open **Automation › Workflows** and create a workflow that starts with **Contact Created**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a webhook">
      Add a **Webhook** action, set its **Method** to **POST**, and paste the link shown on the GoHighLevel row in Auto-import.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tell it which contact">
      Under **Custom Data**, type `contact_id` in the first box and `{{contact.id}}` in the second, exactly as written here. Then publish the workflow.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

The row says when new people are coming in straight away, and what to fix when they are not.

### Treat new arrivals as new leads

Each import that is on has a **Treat new arrivals as new leads** switch, on to start with.

- **On**: each person who comes in the moment they are added gets a first text from Lead Nurture, as someone who just got in touch. If 20 or more arrive at once, AgentNode treats them as a list you imported and sends no first text. People found by the once-a-day check get no first text either.
- **Off**: new people are saved as contacts, and nobody gets a first text.

It only changes who arrives from then on.

## Tags

A tag is a label on a contact: `#zillow-leads`, `#past-clients`, `#open-house`. A contact can carry as many as you need, one for each context they belong to. Pick a tag under **Tags** in the menu on the left to see only the people who carry it.

Open a tag's page and you will find **What assistants should know about #past-clients**: a box for who these people are and how to treat them, such as "referred by the Parks, mostly retirees, prefer calls over texts". Every assistant reads it before texting anyone with that tag, up to the five newest tags with notes for each person. A job's working preferences can also name a tag, so "if #sky-realty, sign off as Sky Realty" applies only to those people. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

A tag named for clients, such as `#past-clients` or `client`, makes whoever carries it a client, and so does a client tag from your CRM: Client Relations takes them up, and Lead Nurture stops reaching out to them.

## Opted-out contacts

When a lead replies **STOP**, AgentNode records the opt-out immediately and permanently. It never texts that person again, and no assistant can pick them up. Opted-out contacts remain visible in your list but are clearly marked.

<Warning>
  Opted-out contacts cannot be texted again from AgentNode. This cannot be undone from within the app.
</Warning>
