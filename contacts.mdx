Contacts is where everyone you have brought into AgentNode lives, with their tags, whether AgentNode manages them, and their conversation beside them. **Add contacts** is how you add people yourself: by hand, from a photo, by voice, or from a CSV file. **Auto-import** is where your CRM and other sources bring people in by themselves.

The list opens with the people AgentNode texted most recently at the top, and the people it has never texted at the bottom. **Sort** puts it in another order, such as by name or by who you added most recently. Filter it too, by **Managed** among other things.

**Last contacted** is when AgentNode last texted someone. It stays empty until AgentNode does, even if your CRM or your CSV file says you were in touch before.

## Managed

**Managed** is whether AgentNode works on a contact. For a managed contact, the assistants text them and answer their texts for you, on their usual schedule and inside your hours. For anyone not managed, AgentNode does nothing on its own: they are saved, and you can still text them yourself.

You choose it as you bring people in. Adding someone by hand, the switch starts on; a CSV file or a CRM import starts off, so an old list is not texted just for arriving. Change it any time after, on their row in **Contacts** or on their page, or in the iPhone app. Switched on from their row, their page or Operator, AgentNode asks if there is anything it should know first, such as "see if they're free on Friday" or "we reconnected last week", and the assistants plan with it. **Skip** it if there is nothing to say. It asks every time, so switching someone back on is the moment to say what changed. See [Adding a single contact](#adding-a-single-contact) and [Importing a CSV](#importing-a-csv).

Switching someone off cancels the texts the assistants had planned for them, and takes what they asked you about them off the Inbox. Switching them on has the assistants start on them straight away, so they may get a text soon; whether it sends by itself or waits for your OK depends on **Settings › Messaging**. If your CRM has them down for later, their first text waits until then, unless you tell AgentNode otherwise when it asks. Nobody who has opted out is texted, managed or not.

To switch many at once, tell [Operator](/operator): "stop managing everyone from Zillow", "manage my past clients again". For a whole group, Operator first tells you how many people it will switch, and switches them only when you say yes.

### Approving first plans

AgentNode never texts a lead before it has a plan for them: which texts the assistants mean to send, and when. To read that plan before anything reaches them, turn on **Manually approve the first plan for each lead** under **Settings › Messaging**. It starts off.

With it on, each lead's first plan waits for you, and nothing reaches them until you approve it. Every plan after that goes ahead on its own, so you are asked once per lead, never again as their plan changes. Leads AgentNode has already texted are past their first plan, so they are not asked about.

Approving a plan is the only yes it needs. Its texts go as planned whatever **Reply for me** or **Confidence** would otherwise hold back, and one due in quiet hours goes the next morning. A meeting in it is booked without asking you again, and the text confirming it goes too. A text still stops if the lead opts out or you switch Managed off. While AgentNode is paused in their conversation, the plan's texts wait for you in your Inbox instead. If the lead writes back, or another text reaches them first, yours included, AgentNode plans again. That new plan follows your settings as usual.

Plans waiting for you are in your Inbox, on the lead's page, and on their row in **Contacts**, where the **Plan needs approval** filter finds them all. Approve one at a time, or all at once: a big list then starts half a minute apart, so it is not texted in one go. A plan that changed since you looked, or that AgentNode is still working on, keeps waiting, and AgentNode says how many did. **Undo** takes an approval back while it shows. Want something changed first? **Update plan** tells the assistants, in your words, what to change, and the new plan waits for you too.

You can also ask for it for just the people one import brings in, with the setting off: a CSV import and each CRM import have their own **Manually approve the first plan for each lead**, off to start with. Switching the setting off later sends nothing that is waiting: those plans still wait for you.

### What managing costs

Your credits go on the work AgentNode does for your managed contacts. For each one, the assistants answer every text they send, check on them while they are quiet (every few days for a new lead, once a month for one gone cold), and write and send their texts. Each of these uses credits, at the rates on the [pricing page](https://goagentnode.com/pricing). People cost the most in their first days: the assistants start on them straight away, and most get a first text.

A contact who is not managed costs nothing while they are quiet: no assistant looks at them or texts them. A text they send you still uses a little, to receive it and learn from it, and so does a text you send them yourself.

So keep Managed on for the people you want AgentNode to work, and off for everyone else, such as an old list you keep for reference. Credits spent on people you don't need followed up are credits a new lead may need. When your credits run out, AgentNode stops working, replies included, until they renew. See [Billing](/settings#billing).

## A contact's page

A contact's page holds everything about them, and the conversation with them. Every link to a conversation, such as a question in the Inbox, opens it.

- **About**: their details, including **How you know them**, their birthday and home anniversaries, **Your note**, any **Details** you added, and the **Summary** AgentNode writes about them (below). The pencil edits them, and whether they agreed to be texted ([Consent](#consent)). Every assistant reads How you know them and your details before it texts them, so it writes the way you would to that person. The assistants read your note too, but never put it in a text.
- **Overview**: their last message beside the next text planned for them, with when it goes, which assistant planned it and why. Under them, **Coming up** lists everything else planned, a line each: later texts, and steps like looking again when they have not replied or asking you whether they came to a meeting. Press a line to see it whole, and **Revise** or **Delete** a text. **Update plan** tells the assistants, in your words, what you want planned or changed. Their **Managed** switch is at the top of the page: at the end of the tabs on a computer, beside **Call** and **Text** on a phone.
- **Conversation**: the whole conversation with them, calls included. Each text says who wrote it: **Automatic** when an assistant sent it on its own, **AI draft** when you sent words AgentNode wrote. A text that did not go says why, with **Retry**. Press the AI logo on one of AgentNode's texts to say what to correct, or why it was wrong.
- **Notes** and **Files**: only you see notes. AgentNode never reads them, so nothing in one can end up in a text.

Above the reply box is the next text an assistant has planned for them and when, or why none is coming. **Send now instead** sends it now, as it is or in your own words, in place of the planned one. Nobody has texted them yet? You write the first text, or pick a suggested opener.

On the web, once anybody has texted them, you can send them photos: press the photo button by the reply box and pick up to 5. On a computer you can also paste them into the box, or drop them on it. They go with your words or on their own. A text with photos costs about 0.06 credits, however long it is.

### Calling a contact

Press the phone on their row, or **Call** on their page. AgentNode checks you mean to call them first, then calls from your number, through your computer or phone: allow the microphone the first time your browser asks. On the call you can mute, use the keypad for a menu that answers, and hang up; closing the tab ends the call too. Afterwards the call is on their conversation, with its recording and transcript once they are ready.

Someone who opted out cannot be called. A call uses credits for the minutes it runs.

### Consent

To change whether they agreed to be texted, press the pencil on **About**, tick or untick **They gave me permission to text them**, and **Save**. Ticked, AgentNode can text them; unticked, nothing is texted to them, and any texts the assistants had planned are called off. Someone who opted out stays unticked, and the box cannot be ticked for them.

## What AgentNode learns about them

AgentNode learns about each contact in the background, as things happen. When a lead texts something about themselves that will still matter in a month, such as a move in March, a new budget or a birthday, AgentNode keeps it. Tell Operator something about a lead, like "Dana's moving to Leeds in March", or write it when you answer a question about them in the **Inbox**, and it is kept too. So is what they say about themselves on a call, once its transcript is ready, and what an assistant learns while it works for them. Every assistant reads it each time it works for them, so what one learns, they all remember. It stays in the background: their page does not list it.

What you see is the **Summary** under **About**: a few sentences on who they are and where things stand, written by AgentNode from what it has learned, the details you added, and their birthday and home anniversaries. AgentNode rewrites it a couple of minutes after any of that changes. A contact it knows nothing about yet has no summary. Writing one uses a little AI, billed in credits like the rest.

You can add details of your own, anything you want on file: a name and a value, such as "Lender" and "Chase". Add, change or remove them under **About** on their page, or ask Operator: "put Lender: Chase on Sam". Every assistant reads them.

AgentNode learns this for every contact, whether or not their Managed switch is on. Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are kept private: the assistants take them into account, but never mention them in a text. Anything you typed yourself stays as you wrote it.

## Importing a CSV

A CSV is a plain spreadsheet file. Every CRM can export one, and so can Google Sheets (**File › Download › Comma Separated Values**) and Excel (**Save As**, then choose CSV). It needs a column of names and a column of mobile numbers; anything else is optional.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your contacts">
    Save a CSV from your CRM, spreadsheet or lead source. AgentNode accepts files of any size, so you do not need to split large lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it from Contacts">
    Press **Add contacts** in Contacts, then **Import from CSV**, and choose the file. Or send the file to [Operator](/operator) with "import these new leads".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the columns">
    AgentNode works out which column is the name, which is the phone number, and which are the email, address, source, tags, birthday, home purchase date and note. Check it got them right. Every other column with something in it is kept for AgentNode to read, such as "Timeframe: 6-12 months": the assistants take it into account, never quote it in a text, and it does not show on the contact's page. Up to 30 columns are kept this way; set any you don't need to **Do not import**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what AgentNode should do with them">
    Answer **Should AgentNode automatically start managing imported contacts?** Neither answer is picked for you, so the import waits until you choose. **Yes** has the assistants start on everyone straight away, so most get a first text. **No** saves them as contacts only, which costs nothing while they are quiet. See [What managing costs](#what-managing-costs).

    With **Yes**, two more questions. **Text them as new leads?** (**No** to start with): say **Yes** if these people just got in touch with you, and Lead Nurture's first text answers them and follows up until they reply or book. **Manually approve the first plan for each lead?** (**No** to start with): say **Yes** to approve each person's first plan before anything is sent to them. See [Approving first plans](#approving-first-plans).

    **How you know these people** is optional: a line like "past clients" goes on everyone in the file, and the assistants text them the way you would, never as strangers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. AgentNode saves your confirmation, and texts nobody without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If a phone number is already in your contacts, AgentNode skips that row and leaves the contact as it is, so uploading the same list twice does not create doubles.
</Note>

## Adding a single contact

Press **Add contacts**, then **Add manually**. Give their name and number, and their source if you know it, and tick that they gave you permission to text them. Then say who they are:

- **A new lead**: Lead Nurture sends the first text and follows up until they reply or book.
- **An old lead or past contact**: Re-engagement checks in with one low-pressure question, with no first-contact intro.

**How you know them** is optional: a line like "friends for years" or "met once at an open house", so the first text reads the way you would write it rather than a stranger's. **Managed by AgentNode** starts on. Switch it off and they are saved as a contact only: nothing is sent until you turn it on. Press **Add contact** to save them.

### From a photo or by voice

Press **Add contacts**, then **Add from image**, and choose a photo: a business card, a screenshot of a contact, or a lead notification. AgentNode reads it and opens the form with their details filled in.

Or press **Add by voice**, then **Start recording**, and say their name and number, and anything else you know, such as what they are looking for. Press **Stop and fill in** when you are done. You can choose an audio file instead, up to 2 minutes long.

Check each detail, then carry on as above. If AgentNode can't find anyone in the photo, the form opens empty for you to type them in. AgentNode never ticks **They gave me permission to text them.** for you: tick it yourself if they agreed. Reading a photo or a recording uses a little AI, billed in credits.

### Through Operator

Or tell [Operator](/operator) who they are: "Add Sam Lee, 555 010 0100, met at Saturday's open house, happy to get texts." Say how you know them, as there, and it goes on their page as **How you know them**. Say that they agreed to hear from you by text. If you don't, they are saved, but not texted until they agree. Say too whether they are a new inquiry, someone who has just reached out to you: only then does Lead Nurture's **New lead** text them, and Operator asks when it cannot tell. They are saved as managed. Nothing is sent the moment they are added: the assistants reach out on their usual schedule.

Or show Operator a screenshot: a contact card, a lead notification, a business card or an email signature. Send it to Operator like a message, on the web or the iPhone, and it adds each person it shows, exactly as written, then tells you what it saved and asks for anything it could not read clearly. A photo doesn't count as permission to text, so say so if they agreed.

## Acting on several people

Tick several people in the list to add them to a list or a tag, delete them, or send them to Operator with what to do. **Send to Operator** opens Operator with their names already there, so you finish the sentence. See [Operator](/operator).

## Auto-import

Auto-import brings people in from your CRM by itself, so you don't have to keep uploading files. New people come in as they are added to your CRM, and once a day AgentNode checks your CRM for anyone it missed. Prime Listing Leads, where you buy leads, is connected for you and checked once a day too: see [Prime Listing Leads](#prime-listing-leads).

The **Auto-import** tab has a card for each source you have connected: whether it is importing, who comes in, how many new people it brought this week and in all, when it last checked, and the tags it gives everyone it brings in. **View imported people** opens them in Contacts. CSV imports and people added through Operator are not listed here.

Under **Add a source** are the ones you have not connected yet, each taking you to **Settings › Integrations**. Your CRM not among them? Export your contacts from it as a CSV and import them once: see [Importing a CSV](#importing-a-csv).

### Switching an import on

1. Connect your CRM once, under **Settings › Integrations**: press **Sign in**, then **Allow**. GoHighLevel also asks which sub-account to connect.
2. Connecting brings nobody in yet. On the **Auto-import** tab, turn on the CRM's switch. **Import contacts** in the CRM's panel under **Settings › Integrations** is the same switch.
3. Answer a few questions, then press **Start importing**. Connecting a CRM during the welcome, or under **Settings › Integrations**, asks the same questions.

The questions are:

- **Import the contacts already in Follow Up Boss?** Either way, new people your CRM adds come in while importing is on. This asks about the people already there. It starts on **No**, because most of a CRM is people nobody wants texted. Choose **Yes** and it asks **Which ones?**: **Everyone** with a mobile number, or **Only some**, picked by stage (Follow Up Boss) or tag.
- **Which tags should they get?** Everyone from your CRM always gets its own tag, such as **Follow Up Boss leads**. Press **Add a tag** to give them more: pick one of your tags, or type a name to make a new one.
- **Bring your Smart Lists too?** Follow Up Boss only. Tick **Yes, bring my Smart Lists across** and each becomes a list in AgentNode. Everyone on them comes in, even people you left out above, and is managed as you answer. A list like "6 Month Follow Up" tells the assistants when to text them. Didn't bring them the first time? **Configure** asks too: **Bring your Smart Lists across?** A list already here with the same name gets back anyone you took off it.
- **Should AgentNode automatically start managing imported contacts?** It starts on **No**: they are saved as contacts only, and nobody is texted. Choose **Yes** and your assistants look after everyone the import brings in. They read what your CRM keeps on each person, such as their timeframe, stage and tags. Someone it has down for later, such as a 6-month follow-up, gets their first text then, not the day they arrive. Someone who has just got in touch still gets a first text straight away: see [Text new people as new leads](#text-new-people-as-new-leads).
- **Text new people as new leads?** Asked when the answer above is Yes. See [Text new people as new leads](#text-new-people-as-new-leads).
- **Manually approve the first plan for each lead?** Asked when AgentNode manages them. It starts on **No**. Choose **Yes** to approve each person's first plan before anything is sent to them: see [Approving first plans](#approving-first-plans).
- **How you know these people** Optional. A line like "past clients from my old brokerage" goes on everyone the import brings in from then on, and the assistants text them the way you would. People already brought in keep what their own page says.
- **Do you have their permission to text them?** Asked the first time only. Tick the box to confirm that the people you bring in agreed to hear from you by text. Anyone your CRM marks as do not contact is skipped.

### Changing an import

Press **Configure** on a CRM's card to change any answer. **Import contacts** has the same **Configure** when you open the CRM in **Settings › Integrations**. While the import is on, both show what the last check found, and **Check now** checks again.

- A tag you add goes on the people already brought in, too. A tag you take off stays on whoever already has it.
- Changing who comes in never removes anyone already brought in.
- Changing whether AgentNode manages them goes for the people brought in from then on. People already brought in keep their own Managed switch.

Switching an import off stops it and keeps everyone already brought in.

### How AgentNode reads your Smart Lists

If you brought your Smart Lists across, **Configure** opens with **How AgentNode reads your Smart Lists**. Under each list is a line saying what AgentNode takes it to mean: who is on it, and when to first text them. "Quarterly Check-in", for example, reads as people to check in with about three months after you were last in touch. Your assistants read the same line for everyone on the list. A list that has only just arrived is read within a few minutes.

If a line is wrong, press **Change** and say how AgentNode should treat the list, such as "Not before January". Your words are marked **Your words**, and the assistants follow them, starting with anyone on the list AgentNode hasn't texted yet. **Use AgentNode's reading** goes back. The same line, with **Change**, is under the list's name when you open it on Contacts.

<Accordion title="Can't sign in? Connect with a key instead">
  Under **Other ways to connect** in Settings › Integrations, you can paste a key instead of signing in.

  - **Follow Up Boss**: in Follow Up Boss, open **Admin › API**, press **Create API Key**, and paste it into AgentNode. If the account owner makes the key, new people come in the moment they are added.
  - **GoHighLevel**: in GoHighLevel, open **Settings › Private Integrations** and create one with **View Contacts** ticked (add **Edit Contacts** and **View Workflows** for AgentNode to add notes, tags and tasks when you ask). Paste it into AgentNode with your **Location ID**, from **Settings › Business Profile**.
</Accordion>

### New people the moment they are added

Follow Up Boss tells AgentNode about new people as soon as they are added, once its account owner has connected it. **Configure** says when new people are coming in straight away, and what to fix when they are not. Without it, new people still come in with the once-a-day check.

GoHighLevel needs one extra step for this. If you would rather not do it yourself, email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com) and we will walk you through it.

<Accordion title="Optional: set up GoHighLevel to send new people straight away">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Create the workflow">

    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a webhook">

### Prime Listing Leads

If you buy leads from Prime Listing Leads, they come into Contacts by themselves. You don't need a key, a password or a press: AgentNode finds your Prime Listing Leads account by the email on your AgentNode account, or your mobile number once it is confirmed in **Settings › Account**, connects it, and switches its import on. Prime Listing Leads then appears on the **Auto-import** tab like any CRM, and under **Settings › Integrations** with the account it found.

- The leads already in Prime Listing Leads when it connects come in tagged **Old lead** beside **Prime Listing Leads**, and **not managed**: your assistants do not text them as if they had just got in touch. Switch Managed on for any you want looked after.
- The leads you buy from then on come in with the once-a-day check, tagged **New lead** and **managed**, so AgentNode looks after them from the start. Nobody gets a first text just for arriving, so **Text new people as new leads?** is not asked.
- Each lead's notes from Prime Listing Leads come in too, and the name of whoever on its team reached out to them. Your assistants read these in the background before they text the lead, and never quote them. They are not added to the contact's **Notes**: you see them in Prime Listing Leads.
- Each lead's address comes in with them.
- Every lead from Prime Listing Leads is a seller. Your assistants know the address is the home they are thinking of selling, and never ask whether they are buying or selling.

If AgentNode finds no account for you, it looks again once a day. To bring it forward, use the same email on both, or email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com) and we will link them; **Connect Prime Listing Leads** under **Settings › Integrations**, and on the welcome's contacts question, looks now. If you switch the import off or disconnect it, AgentNode leaves it that way.

Under **Configure**, on its Auto-import card or in Settings › Integrations, you can narrow who comes in by status, **Active** or **Credits** (the ones you were credited for), and change the tags and Managed for the leads to come. AgentNode only reads Prime Listing Leads, and never changes anything there.

### Text new people as new leads

An import that AgentNode manages asks **Text new people as new leads?** It starts on **Yes**.

- **Yes**: each person who comes in the moment they are added gets a first text from Lead Nurture, as someone who just got in touch. If 20 or more arrive at once, AgentNode treats them as a list you imported and sends no first text. People found by the once-a-day check get no first text either.
- **No**: new people are saved as contacts, and nobody gets a first text.

It only changes who arrives from then on.

## Tags

A tag is a label on a contact: `#zillow-leads`, `#past-clients`, `#open-house`. A contact can carry as many as you need, one for each context they belong to. Pick a tag under **Tags** in the menu on the left to see only the people who carry it, or type its name in **Find a list or tag** at the top of the menu.

A job's working preferences can name a tag, so "if #sky-realty, sign off as Sky Realty" applies only to those people. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

A tag named for clients, such as `#past-clients` or `client`, makes whoever carries it a client, and so does a client tag from your CRM: Client Relations takes them up, and Lead Nurture stops reaching out to them.

## Opted-out contacts

When a lead replies **STOP** (or END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT or STOPALL), AgentNode records the opt-out immediately and permanently. It never texts that person again, and no assistant can pick them up. Opted-out contacts remain visible in your list but are clearly marked.

<Warning>
  Opted-out contacts cannot be texted again from AgentNode. This cannot be undone from within the app.
</Warning>
