Contacts is where everyone you have brought into AgentNode lives, with their tags, who looks after them, and their conversation right beside them. The top bar has two tabs: **Contacts**, with how many people there are, and **Auto-import**, with how many sources are bringing leads in on their own. **Import from CSV** sits at its right.

Open any contact and their page shows their details, their **owner** (Lead Nurture, Client Relations, or you), the jobs they are in with each job's next step, and their conversation.

## What AgentNode learns about them

AgentNode learns about each contact in the background, as things happen. When a lead texts something about themselves that will still matter in a month, such as a move in March, a new budget or a birthday, it goes on their page under **Relationship**, with where it came from: "From Dana's reply, Thu, Oct 8, 9:31 AM". Tell Operator something about a lead, like "Dana's moving to Leeds in March", and it goes there too. Every assistant reads a contact's Relationship before it texts them.

AgentNode learns this for every contact, whether or not their Managed switch is on. Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are marked private: the assistants use them to decide, and never put them in a text. Anything you typed yourself stays as you wrote it, and anything can come off with its **×**.

## Who looks after whom

Every lead has exactly one owner. AgentNode picks it from where the lead came from and what you have told it, and writes down why. Change it on the lead's page, at the head of **About**, and your choice holds. Leads who opt out are looked after by nobody.

## Importing a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your contacts">
    Pull a CSV export from your CRM, spreadsheet, or lead source. AgentNode accepts files of any size, so you do not need to split large lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it from Contacts">
    Press **Import from CSV** in the top bar of Contacts, or the **CSV import** door under **Add a flow** on the Auto-import tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the columns">
    Tell AgentNode which column is the name, which is the phone number, and which columns hold notes it should know about. It remembers your mapping, so future imports from the same format are one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. That record is stored on file, and nobody is texted without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AgentNode deduplicates rows automatically. If a phone number already exists in your contacts, it does not create a second record.
</Note>

## Adding a single contact

Click **Add contact** to bring in one person. Fill in who they are, with their name, phone number, and any notes you want the assistants to know, and they get an owner the same way an imported lead does.

## Acting on several people

Tick people in the list and a bar appears above it: **Send to Operator**, **Add to list**, **Add to tag** and **Delete**. Send to Operator opens Operator with their names already in the ask box, "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: ", so you finish the sentence with what to do. See [Operator](/operator).

## Auto-import

A **flow** is a door leads come in by: Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, a Slack channel, a CSV import, or typed in by hand. The **Auto-import** tab lists each one with how many people it brought in this week and when it last synced, or when the last lead came in, then its **source** and the **tag** it gives everyone it brings in; a CSV gives one tag per file. Who looks after each person is AgentNode's call, as above.

Under **Add a flow** are the doors not open yet, each taking you to where it is connected: a CSV import, Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel and Slack.

### Switching an import on

You connect Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel or Slack once, under **Settings › Integrations**. Connecting imports nobody: its row on Auto-import ends in a switch, and its people come in once you turn it on.

The first time you switch a CRM on, confirm that the people in it agreed to hear from you by text. People it marks do not contact are skipped. While an import is on, its row shows what the last read did, and **Import now** reads it again. Switching it off stops the reads and keeps everyone already imported.

### New leads as they are added

AgentNode reads everyone in a connected CRM once a day. New leads can also come in the moment they are added:

- **Follow Up Boss**: automatic when the connected API key is the account owner's. Any other key works too, and its new people come with the daily read.
- **GoHighLevel**: make a workflow that sends each new contact to AgentNode. The row shows the address to send it to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workflow">
    In GoHighLevel, open **Automation › Workflows** and create a workflow triggered by **Contact Created**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a webhook">
    Add a **Webhook** action with method POST, to the address on the GoHighLevel row in Auto-import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the contact">
    Under its custom data, add `contact_id` with the value `{{contact.id}}`, then publish the workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The row says when new people are arriving as they are added, and what to fix when they are not.

## Tags

A tag is a label on a contact: `#zillow-leads`, `#past-clients`, `#open-house`. A contact can carry as many as you need, one for each context they belong to. Pick a tag under **Tags** in the menu on the left to see only the people who carry it.

Open a tag's page and you will find **What assistants should know about #past-clients**: a box for who these people are and how to treat them, such as "referred by the Parks, mostly retirees, prefer calls over texts". Every assistant reads it before texting anyone with that tag. A job's working preferences can also name a tag, so "if #sky-realty, sign off as Sky Realty" applies only to those people. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

## Opted-out contacts

When a lead replies **STOP**, AgentNode records the opt-out immediately and permanently. It never texts that person again, and no assistant can pick them up. Opted-out contacts remain visible in your list but are clearly marked.

<Warning>
  Opted-out contacts cannot be texted again from AgentNode. This cannot be undone from within the app.
</Warning>
