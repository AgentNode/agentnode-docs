Contacts is where everyone you have brought into AgentNode lives, with their tags, whether AgentNode manages them, and their conversation right beside them. The top bar has two tabs: **Contacts**, with how many people there are, and **Auto-import**, with how many sources are bringing leads in on their own. **Import from CSV** sits at its right.

## Managed

Every contact is **Managed** or not, and the list says which. A managed contact is one AgentNode's assistants text and answer for you; for anyone else, AgentNode does nothing on its own. Everyone you add or import starts out managed. Switch it off at the head of **About** on their page, and back on whenever you like. Nobody who has opted out is texted, managed or not.

To switch many at once, tell [Operator](/operator): "stop managing everyone from Zillow", "turn off managing all contacts", "manage my past clients again". For everyone, a tag or a source, Operator first tells you how many people it will switch, and switches them only when you say yes. Switching someone off cancels the texts the assistants had planned for them. Switching people back on, however many, starts the assistants' work for each of them straight away, so texts can follow for each, sent or held by your auto-send setting.

## A contact's page

Open any contact and their page has three columns:

- **About**: the **Managed** switch, their details, **Your note** and, under **Relationship**, what AgentNode has learned about them. The assistants read your note, and are told never to put it in a text.
- **Conversation**: their latest texts, each captioned **Automatic** when an assistant sent it, **AI draft** when you sent one AgentNode wrote, or **You**. Press the AI logo on one of AgentNode's texts to say what to correct, or why it was wrong.
- **Notes** and **Files**. Only you see notes: AgentNode never reads them, so they cannot end up in a text.

Under the conversation, the **Next** card shows the next text an assistant has planned for them and when, or why none is coming. Its arrow opens **Upcoming messages**: what has happened with them so far, then every text coming. **Send now instead** opens the reply box on that next text: send it as it is or in your own words, and it goes now in place of the planned one, which then does not send.

Below it are **AI drafts**: three replies written from the thread, **Warmer**, **Shorter** and **Ask for a call**. Press one to open the reply box with its words in it, or press **Write a text** to write your own.

## What AgentNode learns about them

AgentNode learns about each contact in the background, as things happen. When a lead texts something about themselves that will still matter in a month, such as a move in March, a new budget or a birthday, it goes on their page under **Relationship**, with where it came from: "From Dana's reply, Thu, Oct 8, 9:31 AM". Tell Operator something about a lead, like "Dana's moving to Leeds in March", and it goes there too, as does what an assistant learns while it works for them: "Noted by Lead Nurture · Follow-up, Thu, Oct 8, 9:31 AM". Every assistant reads a contact's Relationship each time it works for them, so what one learns, they all remember.

AgentNode learns this for every contact, whether or not their Managed switch is on. Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are marked private: the assistants use them to decide, and never put them in a text. Anything you typed yourself stays as you wrote it, and anything can come off with its **×**.

## Importing a CSV

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your contacts">
    Pull a CSV export from your CRM, spreadsheet, or lead source. AgentNode accepts files of any size, so you do not need to split large lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it from Contacts">
    Press **Import from CSV** in the top bar of Contacts, or the **CSV import** door under **Add a flow** on the Auto-import tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map the columns">
    Tell AgentNode which column is the name, which is the phone number, and which columns hold notes it should know about. It remembers your mapping, so future imports from the same format are one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. That record is stored on file, and nobody is texted without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AgentNode deduplicates rows automatically. If a phone number already exists in your contacts, it does not create a second record.
</Note>

## Adding a single contact

Tell [Operator](/operator) who they are: "Add Sam Lee, 555 010 0100, met at Saturday's open house, happy to get texts." Say that they agreed to hear from you by text, or they are saved with no consent on file and nothing is texted to them until there is. Say too whether they are a new inquiry, someone who has just reached out to you: only then does Lead Nurture's **New lead** text them, and Operator asks when it cannot tell. They are saved managed by AgentNode, and nothing is sent the moment they are added: the assistants text them only as their jobs say.

Or show Operator a screenshot: a contact card, a lead notification, a business card or an email signature. Paste it into the ask box, drop it there or pick it with the photo button; on the iPhone, send it with **+**. Operator adds each person it shows, exactly as written, then tells you what it saved and asks for anything it could not read clearly. A photo is never taken as agreement to texts, so say so if they agreed.

## Acting on several people

Tick people in the list and a bar appears above it: **Send to Operator**, **Add to list**, **Add to tag** and **Delete**. Send to Operator opens Operator with their names already in the ask box, "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: ", so you finish the sentence with what to do. See [Operator](/operator).

## Auto-import

A **flow** is a door leads come in by: Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel, a Slack channel, a CSV import, or one at a time through Operator. The **Auto-import** tab lists each one with how many people it brought in this week and when it last synced, or when the last lead came in, then its **source** and the **tag** it gives everyone it brings in; a CSV gives one tag per file.

Under **Add a flow** are the doors not open yet, each taking you to where it is connected: a CSV import, Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel and Slack.

### Switching an import on

You connect Follow Up Boss, GoHighLevel or Slack once, under **Settings › Integrations**. Connecting imports nobody: its row on Auto-import ends in a switch, and its people come in once you turn it on.

The first time you switch a CRM on, confirm that the people in it agreed to hear from you by text. People it marks do not contact are skipped. While an import is on, its row shows what the last read did, and **Import now** reads it again. Switching it off stops the reads and keeps everyone already imported.

### New leads as they are added

AgentNode reads everyone in a connected CRM once a day. New leads can also come in the moment they are added:

- **Follow Up Boss**: automatic when the connected API key is the account owner's. Any other key works too, and its new people come with the daily read.
- **GoHighLevel**: make a workflow that sends each new contact to AgentNode. The row shows the address to send it to.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the workflow">
    In GoHighLevel, open **Automation › Workflows** and create a workflow triggered by **Contact Created**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a webhook">
    Add a **Webhook** action with method POST, to the address on the GoHighLevel row in Auto-import.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the contact">
    Under its custom data, add `contact_id` with the value `{{contact.id}}`, then publish the workflow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The row says when new people are arriving as they are added, and what to fix when they are not. People who arrive as they are added are new inquiries, so Lead Nurture's **New lead** texts them. People from the daily read are not.

## Tags

A tag is a label on a contact: `#zillow-leads`, `#past-clients`, `#open-house`. A contact can carry as many as you need, one for each context they belong to. Pick a tag under **Tags** in the menu on the left to see only the people who carry it.

Open a tag's page and you will find **What assistants should know about #past-clients**: a box for who these people are and how to treat them, such as "referred by the Parks, mostly retirees, prefer calls over texts". Every assistant reads it before texting anyone with that tag, up to the five newest tags with notes for each person. A job's working preferences can also name a tag, so "if #sky-realty, sign off as Sky Realty" applies only to those people. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

A tag named for clients, such as `#past-clients` or `client`, makes whoever carries it a client, and so does a client tag from your CRM: Client Relations takes them up, and Lead Nurture stops reaching out to them.

## Opted-out contacts

When a lead replies **STOP**, AgentNode records the opt-out immediately and permanently. It never texts that person again, and no assistant can pick them up. Opted-out contacts remain visible in your list but are clearly marked.

<Warning>
  Opted-out contacts cannot be texted again from AgentNode. This cannot be undone from within the app.
</Warning>
