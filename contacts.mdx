Contacts is where everyone you have brought into AgentNode lives, with their tags, whether AgentNode manages them, and their conversation right beside them. The top bar has two tabs. **Contacts** lists everyone. **Auto-import** shows the apps, like your CRM, that bring new people in by themselves. **Add contacts** sits at the right, with **Add manually** and **Import from CSV**.

Press **Name** or **Last contacted** at the head of the list to sort by it; press again to reverse it, and a third time for the usual order. **Filters** narrows the list to **Managed** or **Not managed** people, among others. The badges above the list do the common ones in one press, such as **Recently added** for everyone added this week.

**Last contacted** is when AgentNode last texted someone. It stays empty until AgentNode does, even if your CRM or your CSV file says you were in touch before.

## Managed

**Managed** is whether AgentNode works on a contact. For a managed contact, the assistants text them and answer their texts for you, on their usual schedule and inside your hours. For anyone not managed, AgentNode does nothing on its own: they are saved, and you can still text them yourself.

You choose it as you bring people in. Adding someone by hand, the switch starts on; a CSV file or a CRM import starts off, so an old list is not texted just for arriving. Change it any time after, on their row in **Contacts** or on their page, or in the iPhone app. When you switch someone on, AgentNode asks how you know them if you have not said, so its first text reads the way you would write it. See [Adding a single contact](#adding-a-single-contact) and [Importing a CSV](#importing-a-csv).

Switching someone off cancels the texts the assistants had planned for them. Switching them on has the assistants start on them straight away, so they may get a text soon; whether it sends by itself or waits for your OK depends on **Settings › Messaging**. Nobody who has opted out is texted, managed or not.

To switch many at once, tell [Operator](/operator): "stop managing everyone from Zillow", "manage my past clients again". For a whole group, Operator first tells you how many people it will switch, and switches them only when you say yes.

### What managing costs

Your credits go on the work AgentNode does for your managed contacts. For each one, the assistants answer every text they send, check on them while they are quiet (every few days for a new lead, once a month for one gone cold), and write and send their texts. Each of these uses credits, at the rates on the [pricing page](https://goagentnode.com/pricing). People cost the most in their first days: the assistants start on them straight away, and most get a first text.

A contact who is not managed costs nothing while they are quiet: no assistant looks at them or texts them. A text they send you still uses a little, to receive it and learn from it, and so does a text you send them yourself.

So keep Managed on for the people you want AgentNode to work, and off for everyone else, such as an old list you keep for reference. Credits spent on people you don't need followed up are credits a new lead may need. When your credits run out, AgentNode stops working, replies included, until they renew. See [Billing](/settings#billing).

## A contact's page

Open any contact and their page has three columns. **About** is on the left, **Notes** and **Files** are on the right, and the middle has two tabs: **Overview**, which the page opens on, and **Conversation**. **Message** on their row in **Contacts**, and every link to a conversation, such as a notification or a question in the Inbox, opens the **Conversation** tab.

- **About**: their details, including **How you know them**, their birthday and home anniversaries, the **Consent** switch, **Your note**, and any **Details** you added, then the **Summary** AgentNode writes about them (below). The assistants read your note, but never put it in a text. **How you know them** is yours to change behind the pencil, like the rest: every assistant reads it before it texts them, so it writes the way you would to that person.
- **Overview**: whether AgentNode is managing them, such as **Dana is being managed**, with the **Managed** switch beside it. Under it is the newest text in your conversation, who sent it and when, and how many of theirs are new, with **View conversation**. Below that is their plan: every text the assistants have planned for them, soonest first, with when it goes, which assistant planned it and why, each with **Revise** and **Delete**. **Also planned** lists what else the assistants have lined up for them, like looking again when they have not replied or asking you whether they came to a meeting. At the foot, **Update plan** tells the assistants, in your words, what you want planned or changed.
- **Conversation**: the whole conversation with them, newest at the bottom; older texts load as you scroll up. The number on the tab is how many of their texts you have not read. Each text says who wrote it: **Automatic** when an assistant sent it on its own, **AI draft** when you sent words AgentNode wrote. A text that did not go says **Not delivered** and why, with **Retry** under it. Calls show where they happened. Press the AI logo on one of AgentNode's texts to say what to correct, or why it was wrong. The **⋯** at the top of the column clears the chat or deletes the conversation.
- **Notes** and **Files**: the newest five of each. **View all** opens the rest in a panel on the right. Only you see notes: AgentNode never reads them, so they cannot end up in a text.

On a phone, every column is a tab: **Overview**, **Conversation**, **About**, and **Notes & files**.

Under the conversation, the **Next** card shows the next text an assistant has planned for them and when, or why none is coming. **See the plan**, across the foot of the card, takes you to their plan on the **Overview**. **Send now instead** opens the reply box on that next text. Send it as it is or in your own words, and it goes right away instead of the planned one.

Below it, press **Write a text**, or **Text** at the top of the page, to open the reply box. Nobody has texted them yet? The column holds the first text instead: write it, or pick a suggested opener, and press **Start conversation**.

### Calling a contact

Press the phone at the end of their row in **Contacts**, **Call** at the top of their page, or the phone under their conversation. AgentNode asks **Call** and their name first, with their number and the number they will see: yours. Press **Call** and your computer or phone is the phone: allow the microphone the first time your browser asks.

On the call you can **Mute**, open the **Keypad** for a menu that answers, and **Hang up**. Shrink the call to a bar to look at their page or anything else while you talk, and open it again from the bar. Closing or reloading the tab ends the call. When it is over, the call shows on their conversation, with its recording and transcript once they are ready.

Someone who opted out cannot be called. A call uses credits for the minutes it runs.

### Consent

**Consent** in About says whether they agreed to be texted. Switch it on when they have, and AgentNode can text them; switch it off and nothing is texted to them, and any texts the assistants had planned are called off. Someone who opted out stays off, and the switch cannot turn them back on.

## What AgentNode learns about them

AgentNode learns about each contact in the background, as things happen. When a lead texts something about themselves that will still matter in a month, such as a move in March, a new budget or a birthday, AgentNode keeps it. Tell Operator something about a lead, like "Dana's moving to Leeds in March", or write it when you answer a question about them in the **Inbox**, and it is kept too. So is what they say about themselves on a call, once its transcript is ready, and what an assistant learns while it works for them. Every assistant reads it each time it works for them, so what one learns, they all remember. It stays in the background: their page does not list it.

What you see is the **Summary** under **About**: a few sentences on who they are and where things stand, written by AgentNode from what it has learned, the details you added, and their birthday and home anniversaries. AgentNode rewrites it a couple of minutes after any of that changes. A contact it knows nothing about yet has no summary. Writing one uses a little AI, billed in credits like the rest.

You can add details of your own, anything you want on file. On their page, press the pencil beside **About**, then **Add a detail**. Give it a name and a value, such as "Lender" and "Chase", and press **Save**. It shows under **Details** in **About**, and every assistant reads it. Change a value, or take one off with its **×**, the same way. Or ask Operator: "put Lender: Chase on Sam".

AgentNode learns this for every contact, whether or not their Managed switch is on. Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are kept private: the assistants take them into account, but never mention them in a text. Anything you typed yourself stays as you wrote it.

## Importing a CSV

A CSV is a plain spreadsheet file. Every CRM can export one, and so can Google Sheets (**File › Download › Comma Separated Values**) and Excel (**Save As**, then choose CSV). It needs a column of names and a column of mobile numbers; anything else is optional.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Export your contacts">
    Save a CSV from your CRM, spreadsheet or lead source. AgentNode accepts files of any size, so you do not need to split large lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload it from Contacts">
    Press **Add contacts** in the top bar of Contacts, then **Import from CSV**, and choose the file. The import runs in three steps, shown across the top: **Upload**, **Map columns** and **Results**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the columns">
    AgentNode works out which column is the name, which is the phone number, and which hold notes it should know about. Check it got them right. It remembers your choices, so the next import of the same layout is one click.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what AgentNode should do with them">
    Turn on **They are new leads** (off to start with) if these people just got in touch with you. Lead Nurture's first text then answers them, and it follows up until they reply or book. Switch on **Managed by AgentNode** (off to start with) to have the assistants start on everyone straight away, new leads or not, so most get a first text. Left off, they are saved as contacts only, which costs nothing while they are quiet. See [What managing costs](#what-managing-costs). **How you know these people** is optional: a line like "past clients" goes on everyone in the file, and the assistants text them the way you would, never as strangers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm consent">
    Confirm that these are people you have a prior relationship with. AgentNode saves your confirmation, and texts nobody without it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  AgentNode removes duplicates. If a phone number is already in your contacts, it does not create a second record, so you can upload an updated copy of the same list.
</Note>

## Adding a single contact

Press **Add contacts**, then **Add manually**. Give their name and number, and their source if you know it, and tick that they gave you permission to text them. Then say who they are:

- **A new lead**: Lead Nurture sends the first text and follows up until they reply or book.
- **An old lead or past contact**: Re-engagement checks in with one low-pressure question, with no first-contact intro.

**How you know them** is optional, on the same step: a line like "friends for years" or "met once at an open house", so the first text reads the way you would write it rather than a stranger's. **Managed by AgentNode** starts on. Switch it off and they are saved as a contact only: nothing is sent until you turn it on. Press **Add contact** to save them.

Or tell [Operator](/operator) who they are: "Add Sam Lee, 555 010 0100, met at Saturday's open house, happy to get texts." Say how you know them, as there, and it goes on their page as **How you know them**. Say that they agreed to hear from you by text. If you don't, they are saved, but not texted until they agree. Say too whether they are a new inquiry, someone who has just reached out to you: only then does Lead Nurture's **New lead** text them, and Operator asks when it cannot tell. They are saved as managed. Nothing is sent the moment they are added: the assistants reach out on their usual schedule.

Or show Operator a screenshot: a contact card, a lead notification, a business card or an email signature. Paste it into the ask box, drop it there or pick it with the photo button; on the iPhone, send it with **+**. Operator adds each person it shows, exactly as written, then tells you what it saved and asks for anything it could not read clearly. A photo doesn't count as permission to text, so say so if they agreed.

## Acting on several people

Tick people in the list and a bar appears above it: **Send to Operator**, **Add to list**, **Add to tag** and **Delete**. Send to Operator opens Operator with their names already in the ask box, "For Dana, Alina and 2 others: ", so you finish the sentence with what to do. See [Operator](/operator).

## Auto-import

Auto-import brings people in from your CRM by itself, so you don't have to keep uploading files. New people come in as they are added to your CRM, and once a day AgentNode checks your CRM for anyone it missed. Prime Leads Listing, where you buy leads, is connected for you and checked once a day too: see [Prime Leads Listing](#prime-leads-listing).

The **Auto-import** tab lists the CRMs you have connected, one row each. A row shows who comes in, how many people it brought in this week and when it last checked, and the **tags** it gives everyone it brings in. Each row ends with **Configure** and the CRM's switch. CSV imports and people added through Operator are not listed here.

Under **Add an import** are the ones you have not connected yet, each taking you to **Settings › Integrations**.

### Switching an import on

1. Connect your CRM once, under **Settings › Integrations**: press **Sign in**, then **Allow**. GoHighLevel also asks which sub-account to connect.
2. Connecting brings nobody in yet. On the **Auto-import** tab, turn on the CRM's switch. **Import contacts** in the CRM's panel under **Settings › Integrations** is the same switch.
3. Answer a few questions, then press **Start importing**. Connecting a CRM during the welcome, or under **Settings › Integrations**, asks the same questions.

The questions are:

- **Who should come in from Follow Up Boss?** **New people only** brings anyone your CRM adds from now on. **New people, plus some already in Follow Up Boss** also brings the people you pick, by stage (Follow Up Boss) or tag. **New people, plus everyone already in Follow Up Boss** brings everyone with a mobile number. Most of a CRM is people nobody wants texted, so it starts on new people only.
- **Which tags should they get?** Everyone from your CRM always gets its own tag, such as **Follow Up Boss leads**. Press **Add a tag** to give them more: pick one of your tags, or type a name to make a new one.
- **Should AgentNode manage them?** It starts on **No**: they are saved as contacts only, and nobody is texted. Choose **Yes** and your assistants look after everyone the import brings in.
- **Text new people as new leads?** Asked when the answer above is Yes. See [Text new people as new leads](#text-new-people-as-new-leads).
- **How you know these people** Optional. A line like "past clients from my old brokerage" goes on everyone the import brings in from then on, and the assistants text them the way you would. People already brought in keep what their own page says.
- **Do you have their permission to text them?** Asked the first time only. Tick the box to confirm that the people you bring in agreed to hear from you by text. Anyone your CRM marks as do not contact is skipped.

### Changing an import

Press **Configure** on a CRM's row to change any answer, then **Save**. **Cancel** closes it without changing anything. While the import is on, Configure also shows what the last check found, and **Check now** checks again.

- A tag you add goes on the people already brought in, too. A tag you take off stays on whoever already has it.
- Changing who comes in never removes anyone already brought in.

Switching an import off stops it and keeps everyone already brought in.

<Accordion title="Can't sign in? Connect with a key instead">
  Under **Other ways to connect** in Settings › Integrations, you can paste a key instead of signing in.

  - **Follow Up Boss**: in Follow Up Boss, open **Admin › API**, press **Create API Key**, and paste it into AgentNode. If the account owner makes the key, new people come in the moment they are added.
  - **GoHighLevel**: in GoHighLevel, open **Settings › Private Integrations** and create one with **View Contacts** ticked (add **Edit Contacts** and **View Workflows** for AgentNode to add notes, tags and tasks when you ask). Paste it into AgentNode with your **Location ID**, from **Settings › Business Profile**.
</Accordion>

### New people the moment they are added

Follow Up Boss tells AgentNode about new people as soon as they are added, once its account owner has connected it. **Configure** says when new people are coming in straight away, and what to fix when they are not. Without it, new people still come in with the once-a-day check.

GoHighLevel needs one extra step for this. If you would rather not do it yourself, email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com) and we will walk you through it.

<Accordion title="Optional: set up GoHighLevel to send new people straight away">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Create the workflow">
      In GoHighLevel, open **Automation › Workflows** and create a workflow that starts with **Contact Created**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Add a webhook">
      Add a **Webhook** action, set its **Method** to **POST**, and paste the link shown under **Configure** on the GoHighLevel row in Auto-import.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Tell it which contact">
      Under **Custom Data**, type `contact_id` in the first box and `{{contact.id}}` in the second, exactly as written here. Then publish the workflow.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

### Prime Leads Listing

If you buy leads from Prime Leads Listing, they come into Contacts by themselves. You don't need a key, a password or a press: AgentNode finds your Prime Leads Listing account by the email or mobile number on your AgentNode account, connects it, and switches its import on. Prime Leads Listing then appears on the **Auto-import** tab like any CRM, and under **Settings › Integrations** with the account it found.

- The leads already in Prime Leads Listing when it connects come in tagged **Old lead** beside **Prime Leads Listing leads**, and **not managed**: your assistants do not text them as if they had just got in touch. Switch Managed on for any you want looked after.
- The leads you buy from then on come in with the once-a-day check, tagged **New lead** and **managed**, so AgentNode looks after them from the start. Nobody gets a first text just for arriving, so **Text new people as new leads?** is not asked.

If AgentNode finds no account for you, it looks again once a day. To bring it forward, use the same email on both, or email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com) and we will link them; **Connect Prime Leads Listing** under **Settings › Integrations**, and on the welcome's contacts question, looks now. If you switch the import off or disconnect it, AgentNode leaves it that way.

Under **Configure** on its Auto-import row you can narrow who comes in by status, **Active** or **Credits** (the ones you were credited for), and change the tags and Managed for the leads to come. AgentNode only reads Prime Leads Listing, and never changes anything there.

### Text new people as new leads

An import that AgentNode manages asks **Text new people as new leads?** It starts on **Yes**.

- **Yes**: each person who comes in the moment they are added gets a first text from Lead Nurture, as someone who just got in touch. If 20 or more arrive at once, AgentNode treats them as a list you imported and sends no first text. People found by the once-a-day check get no first text either.
- **No**: new people are saved as contacts, and nobody gets a first text.

It only changes who arrives from then on.

## Tags

A tag is a label on a contact: `#zillow-leads`, `#past-clients`, `#open-house`. A contact can carry as many as you need, one for each context they belong to. Pick a tag under **Tags** in the menu on the left to see only the people who carry it.

A job's working preferences can name a tag, so "if #sky-realty, sign off as Sky Realty" applies only to those people. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

A tag named for clients, such as `#past-clients` or `client`, makes whoever carries it a client, and so does a client tag from your CRM: Client Relations takes them up, and Lead Nurture stops reaching out to them.

## Opted-out contacts

When a lead replies **STOP** (or END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT or STOPALL), AgentNode records the opt-out immediately and permanently. It never texts that person again, and no assistant can pick them up. Opted-out contacts remain visible in your list but are clearly marked.

<Warning>
  Opted-out contacts cannot be texted again from AgentNode. This cannot be undone from within the app.
</Warning>
