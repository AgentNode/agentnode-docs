Your account settings are the foundation every text is built from. The name and voice the assistants use, the number they send from, and the hours they may send in all flow from what you configure here. A few minutes getting this right means every text AgentNode sends on your behalf sounds like you, not a bot.

## Account details

Your account is identified by your name and work email address.

- **Name**: the name you use professionally with your clients. The assistants sign off and introduce themselves as you.
- **Work email**: your login identifier. Use the email address you check regularly, as sign-in codes are sent here.
- **Email confirmation**: every new account confirms its email address before it can be used. Enter the code AgentNode sends to your inbox during signup to complete verification.

## Realtor profile

**Settings › Realtor profile** is the assistants' brief on you: your brokerage, your market, how you like to text, and anything you always or never bring up. Every text draws on it, so the more specific you are, the more each text sounds like you.

## Your number

AgentNode sends every text from your own number, not a shared pool. When a lead receives a text from you, it comes from the number they would expect, so replies feel natural and your identity stays consistent across every conversation. Your number is set up as part of account setup and shown in **Settings › Messaging** under **Sending number**.

## Messaging rules

**Settings › Messaging** holds the rules every text follows, whichever assistant wrote it:

- **Always approve the first text to a new contact**: the opener waits in your Inbox until you approve it. Follow-ups send as usual.
- **Quiet hours**: nothing sends in these hours; a text due then waits for morning.
- **Opt-out line on first text**: the first text to each person carries "Reply STOP to opt out".
- **Send on weekends**: whether texts may go out on Saturday and Sunday.

A **Signature** is added to the end of the first text only.

## Changing settings

Open **Settings** from the main navigation to edit your account details, your profile, your number and the messaging rules at any time.

<Note>
  Changes take effect on the next text AgentNode sends. Conversations already in progress are not retroactively changed.
</Note>

## Sign-in options

AgentNode gives you two ways to sign in:

- **Email code**: enter your email address and AgentNode sends you a one-time sign-in code. No password required. This is the default method and works on any device.
- **Password**: if you prefer a traditional login, set a password from your account details page after your email is confirmed. You can then sign in with your email and password instead.

On either method, tick **Keep me signed in** on the login screen to stay signed in across sessions.
