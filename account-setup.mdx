Every text starts from your account settings: the name and voice the assistants use, the number they send from, and the hours they may send in. A few minutes getting this right means every text AgentNode sends on your behalf sounds like you, not a bot.

## Account details

Your account is identified by your name and work email address.

- **Name**: the name you use professionally with your clients. The assistants sign off and introduce themselves as you.
- **Work email**: what you sign in with. Use an email address you check, because sign-in codes are sent there.
- **Email confirmation**: when you sign up, AgentNode emails you a code. Enter it to confirm your email address.

## Realtor profile

**Settings › Realtor profile** is the assistants' brief on you: your brokerage, your market, how you like to text, and anything you always or never bring up. Every text draws on it, so the more specific you are, the more each text sounds like you.

## Your number

AgentNode sends every text from your own number, so leads always see the same number from you. You pick it when you set up your account, and it is shown in **Settings › Messaging** under **Sending number**.

## Messaging rules

**Settings › Messaging** holds the rules every text follows, whichever assistant wrote it:

- **Always approve the first text to a new contact**: the opener waits in your Inbox until you approve it. Follow-ups send as usual.
- **Quiet hours**: nothing sends between 9 PM and 8 AM. A text due then waits in your Inbox for your OK, or you can choose to send it at 8 AM or not send it.
- **Opt-out line on first text**: the first text to each person carries "Reply STOP to opt out".
- **Send on weekends**: whether texts may go out on Saturday and Sunday.

A **Signature** is added to the end of the first text only.

## Changing settings

Open **Settings** from the menu on the left to edit your account details, your profile, your number and the messaging rules at any time.

<Note>
  Changes apply from the next text AgentNode sends. Texts already sent stay as they are.
</Note>

## Sign-in options

AgentNode gives you two ways to sign in:

- **Email code**: enter your email address and AgentNode sends you a one-time sign-in code. No password required. This is the default method and works on any device.
- **Password**: if you would rather use a password, set one in **Settings › Account** after your email is confirmed. You can then sign in with your email and password instead.

On either method, tick **Keep me signed in** on the login screen to stay signed in.
