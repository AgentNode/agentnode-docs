You are set up and your assistants are working. These five things take about ten minutes and make every text sound more like you. All of them live under **Settings**, in the menu on the left.

## 1. Decide what waits for you

**Settings › Messaging** holds the rules every text follows, whichever assistant wrote it.

- **Manually approve the first plan for each lead.** On, each lead's first plan waits in your Inbox, and nothing reaches them until you approve it. Plans after it go on their own. A good way to ease in during your first week. See [Approving first plans](/contacts#approving-first-plans).
- **Reply for me**, under **Replies**, is whether AgentNode answers a lead's texts on its own. It starts on. **Confidence** is how much you trust it to reply without you. It starts on **High**: a reply goes unless AgentNode has real doubt. Set it lower to see more first. Anything it is less sure of waits in your Inbox as a draft for you to send, edit or discard.

## 2. Set your hours

Also under **Settings › Messaging**:

- **Quiet hours**: nothing sends in these hours. You choose what happens to a text that falls in them: it waits for you in the morning, goes at 8 AM on its own, or is not sent. While it waits for you, a reply to a lead who texted in the evening or overnight still goes in the morning, as your **Replies** settings allow.
- **Send on weekends**: off, a text due on Saturday or Sunday waits for Monday. A reply to a lead still goes.

Quiet hours follow your time zone, under **Settings › Account**. AgentNode picks it up from your browser; check it is right.

## 3. Add your facts

- **Settings › Realtor profile**: facts about you and your business, such as your brokerage, your areas, your languages and how long you have been licensed. If AgentNode read your website during setup, some are already there. Your assistants quote a fact when a lead asks, and never volunteer one.

## 4. Tell each assistant how you like things done

Each assistant's jobs are under **Your assistants** in the menu. Open a job and add **working preferences** in your own words: "Offer two viewing times, never an open question about when", "Sign off as Josh, not Sky Realty". Or just tell AgentNode once, "never text before 10", and every assistant works from it. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

When a text was not quite right, press thumbs down under it and say what was wrong. AgentNode learns from it for every assistant. See [How AgentNode learns you](/learning).

## 5. Check your registration and your number

- **Settings › Business** says where your carrier registration stands, in a word: **In review**, **Approved**, or what the carriers want corrected. Approval usually takes 1 to 3 business days, and your number texts in the meantime.
- **Settings › Numbers** lists the number your texts go from. You can buy another here, or release one you no longer need. Your leads text back the number they heard from.

## Signing in

AgentNode signs you in with a code it emails you, so there is no password to remember. Or press **Continue with Apple** or **Continue with Google** and sign in with that account. It opens the AgentNode account with the same email address.

When Apple asks, choose **Share My Email**. With **Hide My Email**, Apple gives AgentNode a different address, and you get a new, empty account.

To sign in with a code by text, save your mobile number first under **Settings › Account**, confirmed by a texted code. From then on, press **Text me a code instead** on the sign-in screen. US numbers only. AgentNode may ask you to sign in again first, to be sure it is you.

If you would rather have a password, set one under **Settings › Account**: AgentNode emails a code to confirm it is you, then you choose one of at least 12 characters. **Keep me signed in** on the sign-in screen keeps you signed in on that device.

**Appearance**, also under Account, sets light or dark mode. **System** follows your device.

<Note>
  Changes take effect on the next text AgentNode sends. Conversations already under way are not rewritten.
</Note>
