The AgentNode iPhone app is Operator in your pocket. Ask it things and tell it what to do, answer what AgentNode is waiting on, read and send texts, and look through your contacts. Everything else, such as importing, your business registration, your profile and your messaging rules, stays on the web, and what you do in either place shows in both.

## Signing in

Sign in as you do on the web: with an emailed or texted code, with Apple or Google, or with your password. The app never sees your password, and you stay signed in until you sign out.

You make your account on the web, at [goagentnode.com](https://goagentnode.com), and it needs an active subscription for the app to show anything.

## What it does

- **Operator.** Ask it anything or tell it what to do, as on the web: "Who replied today?", "Draft a check-in for Carmen". See [Operator](/operator).
- **Clear Inbox.** The questions AgentNode is waiting on, one at a time. Each is one line with its answers, so most take a tap; **See full context** shows what AgentNode read and what it will do with your answer. See [the Inbox](/inbox).
- **Conversations.** Open a lead from a question or from Contacts to read the conversation, send the draft AgentNode wrote, or write your own. Someone you've never written to gets a first text. A lead who hasn't agreed to texts can't be texted.
- **Contacts.** All your contacts, A to Z, searched by name, phone, email or address and filtered to those AgentNode manages or not. Each person's page has their **Managed** switch. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).
- **Speaking.** You can speak instead of typing, and read and change the words before you send. Where it can, your iPhone does this itself, without sending your voice anywhere.
- **Photos and files.** Send up to twenty photos and files with a message to Operator, five of them photos. A screenshot of a contact card sent on its own adds the person on it.

## Notifications

Your iPhone is told when AgentNode has a question for you, when a lead texts you, and when a meeting with a lead is booked, moved or canceled. Tap one to open the question, the conversation or the lead.

Choose which you get in the app's **Settings**: questions from your Inbox, texts from leads, and calendar changes, each on or off. The switches are your account's, so one you turn off stops that kind on every phone you're signed in on.

If your iPhone itself keeps AgentNode's notifications off, the app says so, and takes you to iPhone Settings to allow them.

## Leaving

**Settings › Sign out** ends the app's sign-in and stops its notifications; nothing on your account changes. To delete your account and its data, see [Support](/support#deleting-your-account).
