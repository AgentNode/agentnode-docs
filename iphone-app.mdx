The AgentNode iPhone app is Operator in your pocket. Ask it things and tell it what to do, answer what AgentNode is waiting on, read and send texts, and look through your contacts. Everything else, such as importing, your business registration, your profile and your messaging rules, stays on the web, and what you do in either place shows in both.

## Signing in

Tap **Sign in** and AgentNode's own sign-in page opens over the app. Sign in with the email address you use on the web, with an emailed code or your password, then tap **Allow**. The app never sees your password; it keeps a sign-in you can end from **Settings › Sign out**.

You make your account on the web, at [goagentnode.com](https://goagentnode.com), and it needs an active subscription for the app to show anything.

## What it does

- **Operator.** The first tab opens on a hello and two buttons. **Chat** opens Operator: ask "Who replied today?" or tell it "Draft a check-in for Carmen", and it answers there. See [Operator](/operator).
- **Clear Inbox.** The questions AgentNode is waiting on, one card at a time. Each is one line with its answers, so most take a tap; **See full context** shows what AgentNode read and what it will do with your answer. See [the Inbox](/inbox).
- **Conversations.** Open a lead from a question or from Contacts to read the thread, send the draft AgentNode wrote, or write your own. Someone you've never written to gets a first text. A lead who hasn't agreed to texts can't be texted.
- **Contacts.** The whole book, alphabetical, searched by name, phone, email or address and filtered to those AgentNode manages or not. Each person's page has their **Managed** switch. See [Managed](/contacts#managed).
- **Speaking.** Tap the microphone in any message box and what you say is written into it, to read and change before you send. Recognition happens on your iPhone wherever it can.
- **Photos and files.** The **+** beside Operator's box sends up to twenty photos and files with a message, five of them photos. A screenshot of a contact card sent on its own adds the person on it.

## Notifications

Your iPhone is told when AgentNode has a question for you, when a lead texts you, and when a meeting with a lead is booked, moved or canceled. Tap one to open the question, the conversation or the lead. Choose which you get in **Settings › Channels** on the web, under the iPhone app.

## Leaving

**Settings › Sign out** ends the app's sign-in and stops its notifications; nothing on your account changes. To delete your account and its data, see [Support](/support#deleting-your-account).
