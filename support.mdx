## Contact us

Something wrong in the app? Press **Report an issue and get paid** in the menu on the left, say what happened, and send it. We reply to the email address you sign in with.

Each bug you report that we confirm and fix earns you 3 AgentNode credits ($3), added to your account once the fix is out.

Or send a message from [goagentnode.com/contact](https://goagentnode.com/contact), or email [support@goagentnode.com](mailto:support@goagentnode.com), and a person will answer. To help us help you quickly, include:

- the email address you sign in with
- what you were doing, and what happened instead
- for the iPhone app, the version in **Settings** in the app

For a walkthrough with a person, press **Book onboarding call** in the menu on the left and pick a time.

Most questions about how something works are answered in these docs. New here? Start with the [Quickstart](/quickstart), then [Operator](/operator) and [the Inbox](/inbox). [Words we use](/glossary) explains the terms.

## Signing in

AgentNode signs you in with an emailed code, a texted code, your Apple or Google account, or your password. If an emailed code doesn't arrive, check your junk or spam folder, then ask for another. Or press **Text me a code instead**, which works once your mobile number is saved under **Settings › Account › Sign in by text**. If you've forgotten your password, choose **Use my password instead** and then **Forgot password?**.

**Continue with Apple** opened a new, empty account? You chose **Hide My Email**, so Apple gave AgentNode a different address. Sign in with your email address instead.

The iPhone app signs in with the account you use on the web. If it says your account has no active subscription, check **Settings › Billing** on the web.

## Deleting your account

To delete your account and the data in it, email [legal@goagentnode.com](mailto:legal@goagentnode.com) from the address you sign in with, with the subject line **Privacy Request**, and say you want your account deleted. We verify the request and respond within the time the law requires. See [Privacy and terms](/privacy-and-terms).
