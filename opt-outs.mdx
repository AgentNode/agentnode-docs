When a lead texts STOP, AgentNode stops texting them, straight away and for good. You don't need to do anything or keep a list of who opted out: AgentNode remembers it for your whole account.

## How opt-outs work

<Steps>
  <Step title="Lead sends an opt-out keyword">
    The lead replies with **STOP**, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AgentNode records it straight away">
    It is recorded the moment the reply arrives. You don't need to do anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="It applies to your whole account">
    It covers every assistant, not just the one whose text they replied to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="No more texts">
    No assistant can text them again, and neither can you through AgentNode.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where opted-out contacts appear

Opted-out contacts remain visible in your **Contacts** list. They are clearly marked, and you can still read your past texts with them. The only thing you cannot do is send them a new message through AgentNode.

## Why this matters

US law (the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, or TCPA) says you must stop texting anyone who asks you to. Texting them again without their new permission can mean large fines. AgentNode handles STOP replies for you, so you don't need to track them.

<Warning>
  Never try to reach someone who opted out from another number or another way. That breaks US law and AgentNode's Terms of Service.
</Warning>

## Consent at import

When you import a CSV file, you confirm that everyone in it is someone you already do business with, or who has agreed to hear from you. Only import people who fit that.

If someone has already told you they don't want to hear from you, in any way, don't import them. AgentNode only knows about opt-outs that happen in AgentNode.

<Tip>
  Before you upload a file, take out anyone you know has opted out. AgentNode removes duplicate rows, but it can't know who opted out elsewhere.
</Tip>
