AgentNode learns as you use it. It learns two things: how you like things done, and what each lead tells it. Every assistant uses both, each time it works for a lead. You never have to say the same thing twice.

## Tell it how you like things done

Open **Operator**, or press the **AgentNode** button at the bottom right, and say it in your own words:

- "Call me Josh."
- "Never offer Saturday showings."
- "No texts before 9."
- "I make the calls, AgentNode texts."

AgentNode says it has kept it. From then on, every assistant works that way, for every lead.

A rule you write when you answer a question in the **Inbox**, like "Yes, and never offer Saturdays", is kept the same way.

A one-off request, like "text Dana tomorrow", is done, not kept as a rule. To change how one job works, and not the others, add a **working preference** on that job's page instead. See [Your assistants](/assistants#working-preferences).

## Correct a text, and it learns

Every text an assistant wrote has a thumbs up and a thumbs down under it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press thumbs down on a text that was not right">
    A box opens with the text at the top, and **Needs work** already picked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say what was wrong">
    Pick **Wrong tone**, **Got a fact wrong**, **Too pushy**, **Missed what they asked** or **Something else**. Add a note if you like, such as what it should have said.

    If it looks like a bug rather than a bad text, tick **Report this as a bug to AgentNode**. If we confirm and fix it, you get 3 credits ($3).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Send feedback">
    AgentNode works out the lesson behind it, in a sentence or two, and keeps it for every assistant. Open the same text's feedback again to see it under **What AgentNode learned**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The lesson is about how to write, not about that one lead. "They asked about the HOA, not the taxes" becomes "Answer what the lead asked before anything else", and every text after it, to every lead, is written that way.

A thumbs up with a note teaches it what to keep doing. If AgentNode took the wrong lesson, change your verdict on the same text and it learns again.

<Tip>
  The AI logo beside **AgentNode** on any text it wrote opens the same box.
</Tip>

## It remembers every lead

AgentNode learns about each lead in the background, as things happen:

- **From their texts.** A lead who mentions a move in March, a new budget or a new baby has it kept.
- **From calls.** Once a call's transcript is ready, what the lead said about themselves on it is kept.
- **From you.** Tell AgentNode something about a lead, like "the Hendersons just had a baby", and it is kept for them. So is what you write about them when you answer a question in the **Inbox**.
- **From the assistants.** What an assistant finds out while it works for a lead is kept too.

It keeps what will still matter weeks from now. A time a lead offers for one meeting is not kept: the booking holds it.

All of it stays in the background, off the lead's page. Every assistant reads it each time it works for them, so what one learns, they all know. See [Contacts](/contacts#what-agentnode-learns-about-them).

Sensitive facts, like health or money trouble, are marked private. The assistants use them to decide, and never put them in a text.

## Your way comes first

What you told AgentNode, and what it learned about a lead, beats how it works out of the box. If a job would normally follow up next week but the lead said "not until March", AgentNode waits for March.

Only the guardrails stand above it. Consent and opt-outs, quiet hours, and never stating a fact nobody gave it hold whatever you say. See [Guardrails](/guardrails).

## Change what it knows

- **About a lead**: tell AgentNode what has changed about them, in plain words. Details you added yourself are on their page, under **Details** in **About**. Press the pencil beside **About** to change or take one off.
- **About how you work**: tell AgentNode what has changed, in the same plain words. The newer word wins.
